# Assessment, Badges & Certification

There are no tests in Little Founders. Assessment is evidence-based and criterion-referenced: a
learner shows what they made and says what they learned, measured against published criteria
every learner can meet.

Three principles hold everywhere:

1. **Criterion, not comparison.** Every learner can earn every badge. Nobody's badge is worth
   less because someone else earned it.
2. **Evidence, not assertion.** "I learned about marketing" is not evidence. A poster, a test
   tally, and a sentence about what changed is.
3. **Failure counts.** Several badges can only be earned by something going wrong first. Say this
   out loud, often.

---

## What gets assessed

| Layer | Frequency | Form | Recorded where |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Session log** | Every session | One line: what I did, what I learned | Workbook / platform |
| **Peer feedback** | Every course, S3 | *I like… I wonder… What if…* | Artifact back / feedback log |
| **Course artifact** | Every course, S4 | The main output, against a 3-level rubric | Portfolio |
| **Badge check** | Every course, S4 | Criteria met / not yet | Learning Map |
| **Family note** | After C3, C6, C9, C10 | Short written progress note | Sent home |
| **Portfolio review** | Course 10 | Six curated pieces with captions | Demo Day |
| **Certification** | Course 10 | Tier criteria met | Certificate at Demo Day |

"Not yet" is the only failing grade in the program, and it is always paired with what would make
it a yes.

## The rubric scale

Every rubric uses the same three levels. No fourth level, no numeric score.

| Level | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Emerging** | Attempted; the work does not yet do its job |
| **Secure** | The work does its job. This is the target for every learner |
| **Strong** | The work does its job and shows judgement — evidence, honesty, or independence beyond what was asked |

Secure is the goal. Strong is not a prize and should not be chased: several Strong descriptors
require conditions (a failure, a hard question, a partner who said no) a learner cannot
manufacture.

## Portfolio

The portfolio is the summative assessment. Six pieces, curated by the learner in Course 10, each
with a one-line caption saying what it is and what it shows.

**Required (Junior Builders and up):**

1. A problem artifact from Course 1
2. A plan or canvas from Course 2
3. A cost sheet with a defended price from Course 3
4. A marketing piece from Course 4 with its test results
5. A pitch recording or script from Course 5
6. The Startup Report or Venture Report from Course 9

**Required at every tier:** at least one piece that did not work, with what was learned.

**Little Sprouts** substitute a four-page founder book with three chosen pieces attached.

Format is the learner's choice — folder, box, slide deck, paper. The captions are not optional.

## Certifications

Each tier awards one certification at Demo Day. It is that tier's completion award and carries 50
Little Points.

| Tier | Certification | Requires |
|---|---|---|
| 🌱 Little Sprouts (5–7) | **Junior Founder** | All ten courses; founder book; presented at Demo Day |
| 🔨 Junior Builders (8–10) | **Certified Builder** | All ten courses; six-piece portfolio including a failure; 3-minute talk |
| 🚀 Young Founders (11–13) | **Certified Young Founder** | All ten courses; portfolio; venture reported against a stated target; 4-minute talk |
| 👑 Founder CEOs (14–17) | **Certified Young Entrepreneur** | All ten courses; full portfolio; reasoned continue/pivot/stop decision; 90-day plan; 5-minute talk with Q&A |

Certificates name the learner and the certification: *Mia — Certified Builder, Little Founders*.
A learner short of the full ten courses receives the **Future Founder** badge on the evidence they
hold and may complete the certification in a later cohort.

## Little Points

Little Points (*Petits Points*) measure participation and effort — the things a learner controls —
never quality. They advance the Learning Map and unlock courses.

### Earning

| Action | Points |
|---|---|
| Complete a session (present and produced the artifact) | 10 |
| Earn any badge | 25 |
| Earn the tier certification | 50 |
| Market Day (Course 9) | 50 |
| Demo Day (Course 10) | 50 |

Full-tier maximum: 420 session points + 100 event points + 725 badge and certification points =
**1,245 lifetime points**.

### Balance vs. lifetime

The platform shows two numbers; do not confuse them.

- **Lifetime earned** — never decreases. Used for recognition and unlock gates.
- **Balance** — lifetime earned minus points spent on rewards. This is why a learner's visible
  balance is often lower than what they have earned in total.

### Spending

| Reward | Cost |
|---|---|
| Choose the warm-up game for a session | 25 |
| Choose your team's role for the next course | 50 |
| Physical badge pin rather than a sticker | 100 |
| Name a class business, or design the cohort's stall banner | 150 |

Points are never deducted as punishment. A behaviour system that spends children's earned credit
teaches that effort is confiscatable.

### Unlock gates

| Course | Requirement |
|---|---|
| 1–5 | Open |
| 6 Leadership Lab | Courses 1–5 complete **and** 250 lifetime points |
| 7 Young CEO | Course 6 complete |
| 8 Community Builder | Course 7 complete |
| 9 Startup Challenge | Courses 1–8 complete + **Money Smart** + **I Can Pitch!** |
| 10 Future Founder Mastery | Course 9 complete |

The 250-point gate is set so finishing Courses 1–5 (200 points) plus any two badges clears it. A
learner who has genuinely done the first five courses will not be blocked. If a whole cohort is
blocked, the Guide has been awarding session points too strictly.

### Award rules

- **Award publicly at the end of Session 4, every course.** Thirty seconds per learner is enough.
- **Criteria are published to learners in advance**, printed on the back of the course divider.
  Hidden criteria are not criteria.
- **"Not yet" always comes with a route** — name the one thing that would make it a yes, and when.
- **Never award a badge to soften something.** Children detect it immediately, and it devalues
  every badge in the room.
- **Badges can be earned late.** A learner may claim one in any later course by bringing evidence.
- **Keep Building is the badge to be generous with** — but only where a real failure preceded it.

## Guide's assessment record

One page per learner per course:

```
Learner:                                Course:                Tier:
Sessions attended:      /4               Artifact received: Y / N
Rubric: Problem ___  Evidence ___  Change ___  Voice ___     (E / S / St)
Badges awarded:
Not yet — and the route:
Note for family (one sentence):
```

Ten minutes per course for a group of twelve, if you fill it during Session 4's Work block while
learners are working. Filling it afterwards from memory takes three times as long and is less
accurate.

## Moderation

Where more than one Guide runs the program, moderate twice a year:

1. Each Guide brings three anonymised artifacts — one judged Emerging, one Secure, one Strong.
2. Others judge them blind against the same rubric.
3. Discuss only the disagreements. Adjust the shared understanding, not the rubric.

Most disagreement is about *evidence*, not quality: one Guide accepts a claim, another asks "how
do you know?" Converge on asking.

## Reporting to families

Family notes go home after Courses 3, 6, 9, and 10. Each is three sentences plus a question:

1. What your child did.
2. What they can do now that they could not before.
3. What is next.
4. One question to ask at dinner.

No grades, no comparison to other learners, no "could try harder." Samples appear at the end of
each course file. In bilingual cohorts the note goes home in the family's preferred language.

## Accessibility of assessment

Every route has an equivalent that does not require writing, speaking aloud, or presenting to a
large group. These are equivalent routes, not concessions, and carry identical credit:

| Instead of | A learner may |
|---|---|
| Writing an artifact | Draw it, record it, or dictate it to a scribe |
| Presenting to a room | Present to one person, pre-record, sign, or have a partner read while they hold the prop |
| A spreadsheet | Use a paper tracker, or a template with formulas supplied |
| Making a real ask in person | Send a written ask |
| Reading a handout | Have it read aloud, or use the picture version |
| Working in English | Work, pitch, and write in French, with a one-line summary in the other language |

The criterion is always the *capability*, never the format it arrives in.
