# Course 9 — Startup Challenge

**Icon:** Rocket · **Colour:** Energy · **Core badge:** Launch Crew · **Sessions:** 6
**Prerequisites:** Courses 1–8 complete; **Money Smart** and **I Can Pitch!** held
**Français:** Défi Startup

> Build it. Sell it. Learn from it.

## Big idea

Everything so far has been preparation. This is the real thing: a product, real customers, real
money from Junior Builders upward, and a public deadline that does not move. Two cycles of
Notice–Make–Show–Change run back to back, so a bad first day is recoverable.

**Milestone:** Market Day sits between Session 4 and Session 5.

## Essential questions

- Can we actually deliver what we promised, on the day, to strangers?
- What did the real world tell us that our plan did not?
- What would we do differently with one more week?

## Objectives by tier

| | A learner can… |
|---|---|
| 🌱 **Little Sprouts** | Take part in a class shop with a job they hold, sell to a real visitor, and say what they learned |
| 🔨 **Junior Builders** | Run a stall from a $50 float, hold a role, serve real customers, keep the money right, and report profit honestly |
| 🚀 **Young Founders** | Run a validated venture from a $100 float, hit or miss a stated target, and explain the gap between forecast and actual |
| 👑 **Founder CEOs** | Launch a venture with real customers, operate to plan, keep an accurate ledger, adapt mid-sprint, and report unit economics against forecast |

## Vocabulary

| Term | Plain meaning | From tier |
|---|---|---|
| Stall / shop | Where we sell | Sprouts |
| Customer | Someone who really buys | Sprouts |
| Float | The money we start with | Builders |
| Stock | How many we have to sell | Builders |
| Takings | All money received | Builders |
| Target | The number we said we would hit | Founders |
| Sprint | A short push with a fixed end | CEOs |
| Actual vs. forecast | What happened vs. what we said would happen | CEOs |

## Sprint shape (all tiers)

| Session | Beat | Focus |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | NOTICE | Final prep: what we sell, to whom, at what price, who does what |
| 2 | MAKE | Production and setup — make the stock, build the stall, write the signs |
| 3 | SHOW | Dry run with a friendly audience; fix what breaks |
| 4 | CHANGE | Fix, restock, final checks, money and safety brief |
| — | **MARKET DAY** | Real customers, real money |
| 5 | NOTICE | Count everything: money, stock, feedback, what happened |
| 6 | CHANGE | Reconcile, analyse, decide what you would do next |

**The dry run in Session 3 is non-negotiable.** Every failure that appears on Market Day appeared
in the dry run first, in a form somebody chose to ignore.

## 🌱 Little Sprouts (5–7) — 6 × 45 min

### S1 · NOTICE — What are we selling?
Whole-group picture voting: what we sell (2–3 items maximum), what each costs in coins, and who
does which job. Course 6 job cards return. **Artifact:** job card + product list.

### S2 · MAKE — Making the stock
*Production Line* warm-up. Set a group target ("we need 30") and count on a big chart. One Helper
plays the customer and inspects quality. **Artifact:** stock counted against target.

### S3 · SHOW — Dry run
Practise the words: "Hello, would you like…" · "Thank you, that's two coins." Another class visits
with play coins. Everyone does their job; Helpers note what went wrong. **Artifact:** dry run +
three fixes chosen by the group.

### S4 · CHANGE — Fix and finish
Apply the three fixes, finish stock, make final signs, set out the shop. Invitations home; shop
rules read aloud together. **Artifact:** shop ready.

### 🚀 MARKET DAY — Class Shop (45–60 min)
Real visitors — families, other classes, staff. Adults handle any real money; learners handle
tokens or supervised coins. Every learner does their job for the whole session.

### S5 · NOTICE — Count it up
Count takings, leftover stock, and visitors. Build one big picture chart of all three.
**Artifact:** three-count chart.

### S6 · CHANGE — What we learned
Draw *Next Time* pages: one thing to keep, one to change. Decide together what happens to the
money using the Course 3 envelopes. **Artifact:** *Next Time* page + money decision.
Badge check: **Launch Crew**, **Young CEO**, **Keep Building**.

## 🔨 Junior Builders (8–10) — 6 × 60 min

This is the tier the poster promises will *build, make and sell real products*. Market Day is the
centre of their year.

### S1 · NOTICE — Final plan and float
- **Mini-Lesson (10):** Lock the plan: product, price (Course 3), quantity, roles (Course 6),
  marketing (Course 4), give-back (Course 8). Then the float: up to $50 CAD, signed out, every
  dollar accounted for.
- **Work (35):** Complete the one-page Market Day plan. Calculate how many units break even, set a
  stretch target, sign out the float with two signatures.
- **Artifact:** Market Day plan + float signed out.

### S2 · MAKE — Production and stall build
- **Warm-Up (5):** *Batch It.* Time one made alone vs. four made in a batch.
- **Mini-Lesson (10):** Time per unit × units needed = the hours you do not have. Decide what to
  cut now, not the night before.
- **Work (35):** Produce against a tracked target; build the stall layout and signage. The Money
  role sets up the tin, float count, and takings sheet.
- **Artifact:** stock ≥ 60% of target + stall built.

### S3 · SHOW — Dry run with real feedback
- **Warm-Up (5):** *Awkward Customer.* Someone haggles, someone wants a refund, someone has no
  change.
- **Work (35):** Run the stall for 20 minutes with a friendly audience, using real small money
  where permitted. Hard debrief: what broke, what confused people, what took too long.
- **Artifact:** dry run takings + three ranked fixes.

### S4 · CHANGE — Fix, restock, brief
- **Mini-Lesson (10):** The money brief — who handles cash, two-person counting, where the tin
  lives, what to do if the amount is wrong. Then the safety brief per policy.
- **Work (35):** Apply fixes, finish stock, final pricing check, print signs, pack the kit box to
  a checklist.
- **Artifact:** signed kit checklist + fixes applied.

### 🚀 MARKET DAY — Stall (90–120 min)
Roles held throughout with one rotation at the midpoint. Money counted at open, midpoint, and
close — two learners count, one adult witnesses, all three initial. One line of notes every 20
minutes: what sold, what people asked, what they walked away from.

### S5 · NOTICE — Reconcile and analyse
- **Mini-Lesson (10):** Float + takings − spend = what should be in the tin. If it does not match,
  you find out why; you do not adjust the number.
- **Work (35):** Count and reconcile. Calculate actual profit, actual cost per unit, and units sold
  vs. break-even. Chart forecast vs. actual.
- **Artifact:** reconciled accounts + forecast vs. actual chart.

### S6 · CHANGE — Learn and decide
- **Mini-Lesson (10):** Separating luck from skill: what would have happened with different
  weather, a different spot, a different day? Then apply the split rule and deliver the give-back.
- **Work (35):** Write the **Startup Report**: what we sold, what we made, what we learned, what we
  would change, what we did with the money.
- **Artifact:** Startup Report + give-back delivered.
Badge check: **Launch Crew**, **Young CEO**, **Money Smart**, **Keep Building**, **Team Player**,
**Community Builder**.

## 🚀 Young Founders (11–13) — 6 × 75 min

Young Founders launch against a **stated target** written before Market Day. The learning is in
the gap between what they predicted and what happened.

### S1 · NOTICE — Lock the venture and the target
- **Mini-Lesson (12):** Review the Course 7 sprint plan. Set the public target — units, revenue,
  and one customer measure — written on the wall where it cannot move. Float up to $100 CAD.
- **Work (42):** Finalise offer, price, channels, production plan, roles and rotation, and the
  give-back. Run the legal and safety checks (food, permission to trade, data) — see safeguarding.
- **Artifact:** locked plan + public target + float signed out.

### S2 · MAKE — Production and marketing live
- **Warm-Up (8):** *Critical Path.* Which task, if late, delays everything?
- **Mini-Lesson (12):** Bottlenecks and buffer; quality standard written before production starts,
  or it drifts under time pressure.
- **Work (42):** Produce against the plan. Marketing goes out per the Course 4 plan with response
  tracking. Pre-orders open where the offer allows.
- **Artifact:** stock in progress + marketing running with a response count.

### S3 · SHOW — Dry run at real scale
- **Warm-Up (8):** *Failure Injection.* Remove one team member for ten minutes. Does it survive?
- **Work (42):** Run the stall for 30 minutes with a real audience, real money where permitted.
  One member does nothing but observe and record: conversion (asked vs. bought), time per
  transaction, objections, quality failures.
- **Artifact:** dry run data + objection list.

### S4 · CHANGE — Two changes, then final checks
- **Mini-Lesson (12):** Change two things, both justified by dry-run data. Changing everything
  means learning nothing.
- **Work (42):** Apply the changes, finish stock, run the money and safety brief, update the
  forecast with what the dry run taught, pack the kit to a checklist.
- **Artifact:** two justified changes + updated forecast + signed checklist.

### 🚀 MARKET DAY — Stall (2 hours)
Roles rotate on schedule. Money counted at open, midpoint, close. Dashboard updated hourly
against the target. Continuous customer observation by one team member at all times.

### S5 · NOTICE — Reconcile and find the gap
- **Mini-Lesson (12):** Reconciliation, then variance: for each measure, actual minus target, and
  the most likely cause. Variance is explained, never rounded away.
- **Work (42):** Reconcile to the cent. Calculate actual profit and margin. Compare every number to
  the target written in Session 1. Analyse the customer observations for patterns.
- **Artifact:** reconciled accounts + variance analysis.

### S6 · CHANGE — Report and decide
- **Work (42):** Write the **Venture Report** (2 pages): target, actual, variance, what we learned,
  what we would change. Apply the split rule; deliver the give-back with its measured result. Then
  decide: run it again, change it, or stop — with the reason.
- **Artifact:** Venture Report + recorded decision + give-back delivered.
Badge check: **Launch Crew**, **Young CEO**, **Money Smart**, **Keep Building**, **Deal Maker**,
**Community Builder**, **Green Founder**.

## 👑 Founder CEOs (14–17) — 6 × 90 min

### S1 · NOTICE — Lock the sprint
- **Warm-Up (10):** *Scope Cut.* Cut 30% of your plan right now. What goes?
- **Mini-Lesson (15):** Fixed end date, fixed budget, variable scope. Review the Course 7 operating
  plan including the stop-rule; set the sprint's three numbers.
- **Work (50):** Finalise offer, price, channel, production plan, roles and rotation, cash plan,
  and legal and safety checks — food handling, permission to trade, data rules. Confirm venue and
  Course 8 partnership terms. Float up to $150 CAD.
- **Artifact:** locked sprint plan with stop-rule.

### S2 · MAKE — Build and go live
- **Warm-Up (10):** *Critical Path.*
- **Mini-Lesson (15):** Bottlenecks and buffer; the written quality standard.
- **Work (50):** Production, channel setup, pre-orders open. Ledger live from the first
  transaction. Marketing out per plan with response tracking.
- **Artifact:** stock/service ready + live ledger + marketing running.

### S3 · SHOW — Soft launch
- **Warm-Up (10):** *Failure Injection.*
- **Mini-Lesson (15):** A soft launch is a test with real stakes and a small blast radius: real
  customers, real money, small volume, where failure costs you an apology rather than a
  reputation.
- **Work (50):** Serve real customers at low volume. Capture conversion, time per transaction,
  objections, quality failures. One member observes and records only.
- **Artifact:** soft launch data + objection list.

### S4 · CHANGE — Iterate and scale
- **Warm-Up (10):** *Price Check.* Given soft-launch data, does the price move?
- **Mini-Lesson (15):** Two changes maximum between soft launch and full launch, both justified by
  data.
- **Work (50):** Apply the changes. Scale production. Update the cash plan with actuals. Full
  operational rehearsal end to end, with timings. Confirm give-back logistics.
- **Close (15):** Check the stop-rule honestly — is any team past it?
- **Artifact:** two justified changes + rehearsal + updated cash plan.

### 🚀 MARKET DAY — Full launch (half day)
Public trading. Roles rotate on schedule. Ledger updated live, not reconstructed afterwards.
Hourly dashboard reading. Two-person money handling with adult witness. Continuous customer
observation. Photo and video per the consent list.

### S5 · NOTICE — Reconcile, measure, hear it
- **Warm-Up (10):** *Forecast vs. Feeling.* Write what you think happened, then look.
- **Mini-Lesson (15):** Full reconciliation and honest variance analysis: revenue, unit cost,
  contribution, cash position, conversion, time.
- **Work (50):** Reconcile the ledger to the cent. Calculate actual unit economics against the
  Course 3 model. Analyse observations for patterns. Calculate the response rate per channel.
- **Artifact:** reconciled ledger + variance analysis + customer insight summary.

### S6 · CHANGE — Report and decide
- **Warm-Up (10):** *Continue, Pivot, or Stop?* Gut answer, written and sealed.
- **Mini-Lesson (15):** An honest venture report: what we set out to do, what happened, what the
  numbers say, what we learned, what we recommend. Bad news goes near the top.
- **Work (50):** Write the **Venture Report** (2 pages max). Apply the split rule; deliver the
  give-back with measured impact. Make and record the continue/pivot/stop decision against the
  stop-rule set in Session 1 — then compare it to the sealed gut answer.
- **Artifact:** Venture Report + recorded decision + give-back delivered.
Badge check: **Launch Crew**, **Young CEO**, **Money Smart**, **Keep Building**, **Deal Maker**,
**Community Builder**, **Green Founder**.
**Mentor touchpoint:** a mentor reads two Venture Reports and responds to the decision, not the
results.

## Market Day operations

### Before

- Venue, tables, power, weather plan, and permissions confirmed one week ahead.
- Risk assessment per the setting's policy. **Food ventures need provincial and municipal food
  rules checked in Session 1** — this is not optional and can end a venture concept.
- Float signed out, denominations checked, cash tin and secure location agreed.
- Consent list checked for photography.
- Adult ratios: Sprouts 1:6, Builders 1:8, Founders 1:10, CEOs 1:12, plus one adult on money at
  all times.

### During

- Money counted at open, midpoint, and close. Two learners count, one adult witnesses, all three
  initial the sheet.
- No learner is ever alone with the tin or with an unfamiliar adult.
- Every team logs one observation line every 20 minutes.
- The Guide does not sell, price, or rescue. The Guide keeps people safe and takes photographs.

### After

- Reconcile the same day if possible; memory decays fast and disputes grow.
- Leftover stock is logged, not binned quietly — it is data about forecasting.
- Thank every partner, visitor, and helper within a week (Course 8 skill, applied).

## Evidence & badge criteria

| Badge | Criteria | Points |
|---|---|---|
| **Launch Crew** (core) | Held a role through the full sprint and traded with real customers on Market Day | 25 |
| **Young CEO** | Made a decision during the sprint based on live data | 25 |
| **Money Smart** | Money reconciled accurately, with any discrepancy explained | 25 |
| **Keep Building** | Something failed; they fixed it and went again within the sprint | 25 |
| **Team Player** | Covered for a teammate or handed over cleanly under time pressure | 25 |
| **Community Builder** | Delivered the give-back as planned | 25 |

Session completion: 10 Little Points each (60 for the course). Market Day: 50 points.

**Rubric — the venture:**

| | Emerging | Secure | Strong |
|---|---|---|---|
| Delivery | Did not trade, or adults carried it | Traded with real customers | Traded and adapted live to what was happening |
| Money | Not reconciled | Reconciled, gaps explained | Accurate to the cent; variance analysed against forecast |
| Learning | "It was fun" | Names what worked and what did not | Traces outcomes to specific decisions, including their own errors |
| Teamwork | Roles collapsed | Roles held | Roles held and flexed; gaps covered without instruction |
| Integrity | Overclaimed results | Reported accurately | Reported bad news first and unprompted |

## Differentiation & access

- **Learners overwhelmed by public trading:** Behind-the-scenes roles — production, stock, money,
  observation, restocking — are full **Launch Crew** roles. Customer-facing is not required.
- **Sensory load on Market Day:** Plan a quiet station and a scheduled break in advance, not
  improvised on the day.
- **Teams whose product fails in the dry run:** They pivot in Session 4 to a simpler offer. This is
  the best possible learning outcome and should be celebrated, not treated as a setback.
- **No trading permitted in your setting:** Run Market Day with an internal token economy. Every
  badge except the money-reconciliation criterion applies unchanged; substitute a token
  reconciliation.
- **Bilingual Market Day:** Signage in both languages, and each team decides in advance who serves
  in which language. Learners consistently report this as the moment bilingualism felt useful
  rather than assigned.
- **Extension (CEOs):** Run a second trading occasion with the changes applied and compare the two
  sets of unit economics.

## Guide watch-outs

- **Adults running the stall.** The most common failure of this course. It will be slower,
  scruffier, and less profitable when learners run it. That is the correct outcome.
- **Skipping the dry run to make more stock.** Every year, someone asks. Refuse. Under-stocked and
  rehearsed beats fully-stocked and chaotic.
- **Money going missing.** Almost always a process failure, not theft. Two-person counting at three
  fixed points prevents nearly all of it. Never accuse; investigate the process out loud and fix
  it.
- **Food safety assumed rather than checked.** Check provincial and municipal rules in Session 1.
  Discovering it the night before is a preventable disaster.
- **Profit becoming the whole point.** The team that lost money and can explain exactly why has
  learned more than the team that sold out by luck. Say this *before* Market Day, or it sounds
  like consolation.
- **The exhausted crash.** Market Day is emotionally enormous. Session 5 starts with feelings
  before numbers, and expect some tears in every cohort.
- **Comparing teams publicly on takings.** Do not. Badges are criterion-based for exactly this
  reason.

## Home extension

**🌱 Sprouts:** *Tell the Story.* Tell someone at home what your job at the shop was and what
happened.

**🔨 Builders:** *Family Debrief.* Explain your takings, costs, and profit to a family member. Can
they follow it?

**🚀 Young Founders:** *Target vs. Actual.* Tell someone at home the number you promised and the
number you got. Explain the gap.

**👑 Founder CEOs:** *The Honest Version.* Tell someone at home the part that went wrong and what
you would do differently. Practising honest reporting out loud makes the written report better.

## Family note (after Session 6)

> Your child built something, sold it to real people, handled real money, and then sat down and
> worked out what actually happened. Some of it went wrong. That is the part that teaches.
>
> **Ask at dinner:** *"What went wrong on Market Day, and what did you do about it?"*
>
> If they can answer that clearly, they have learned more this month than any profit figure would
> show.
