# Course 7 — Young CEO

**Icon:** Crown · **Colour:** Teal · **Core badge:** Young CEO · **Sessions:** 4
**Prerequisites:** Course 6 · **Français:** Jeune PDG

> Having the idea is the easy part. Running it is the job.

## Big idea

A CEO's real work is a loop: decide what matters, watch a small number of honest numbers, make a
call, and be accountable for what happens next. Learners have made things and led people — this
course is about steering.

## Essential questions

- If I could only see three numbers each week, which three?
- What does this number tell me to *do*?
- What do I do when the number says the thing I did not want to hear?

## Objectives by tier

| | A learner can… |
|---|---|
| 🌱 **Little Sprouts** | Say what the person in charge does, check on their own job, and make one choice for their group and explain it |
| 🔨 **Junior Builders** | Choose and track three numbers weekly and say what went up and what went down |
| 🚀 **Young Founders** | Set a goal with a number and a date, build a simple dashboard, and make one decision the data caused |
| 👑 **Founder CEOs** | Choose metrics that matter, run a weekly review against targets, decide add/cut/keep on evidence, and own the outcome publicly |

## Vocabulary

| Term | Plain meaning | From tier |
|---|---|---|
| In charge | The person who decides and checks | Sprouts |
| Checking | Looking to see if it worked | Sprouts |
| Measure | A number that shows how we are doing | Builders |
| Goal | What we are trying to reach, by when | Founders |
| Weekly review | The meeting where we look at the numbers | Founders |
| Dashboard | The few numbers, in one place | Founders |
| Leading / lagging | Predicts the future / reports the past | CEOs |
| Trade-off | Choosing one thing means giving up another | CEOs |
| Accountability | The outcome is mine to answer for | CEOs |

## 🌱 Little Sprouts (5–7) — 4 × 45 min

### S1 · NOTICE — What does the boss do?

- **Warm-Up (5):** *Boss or Not?* Guide describes actions ("shouts at everyone", "checks everyone
  has what they need"); thumbs vote.
- **Mini-Lesson (7):** The person in charge **decides**, **checks**, and **helps**. Three hand
  actions.
- **Work (25):** **Captain for the Day.** Teams of four; one is Captain for a 10-minute build,
  then hands over. Two rounds, two captains. Helpers prompt quietly.
- **Close (8):** What was hard about being Captain?
- **Artifact:** Captain's three-action card, ticked.

### S2 · NOTICE — Counting how we did

- **Warm-Up (5):** *Count It.* Count something in the room three ways.
- **Mini-Lesson (7):** Grown-ups in business count things to see if their idea is working. We can
  count too.
- **Work (25):** **Our Three Counts.** Pick three things to count during a 15-minute pretend shop
  and tally on a big picture chart.
- **Close (8):** Which count surprised you?
- **Artifact:** Picture tally chart.

### S3 · SHOW — Look at it together

- **Warm-Up (5):** *Bigger or Smaller?* Compare two bar heights.
- **Mini-Lesson (7):** Looking at your counts helps you choose what to do next.
- **Work (25):** **Team Check-In** at the chart. Three questions: what went up? what went down?
  what shall we do differently? Answers drawn on a *Next Time* card.
- **Close (8):** Read the cards aloud.
- **Artifact:** *Next Time* card.

### S4 · CHANGE — Make the change

- **Warm-Up (5):** *Same Shop, New Plan.*
- **Mini-Lesson (7):** A good boss actually does the thing they decided.
- **Work (25):** Re-run the pretend shop with the change. Count the same three things and compare
  charts side by side.
- **Close (8):** Badge check: **Young CEO**, **Number Cruncher**.
- **Artifact:** Two charts, before and after.

## 🔨 Junior Builders (8–10) — 4 × 60 min

### S1 · NOTICE — Three numbers

- **Warm-Up (5):** *Which Number Matters?* A stand sold 40 cups but lost money. Which number did
  they watch, and which should they have?
- **Mini-Lesson (10):** Three measures — one for **money** (profit so far), one for **customers**
  (how many), one for **work** (things made). Define exactly how each is counted.
- **Work (35):** Choose the three, write the definitions, and enter what is already known from
  Courses 3–5.
- **Close (10):** Post definitions on the wall. They stay up through Course 9.
- **Artifact:** Three defined measures with starting values.

### S2 · MAKE — Build the tracker

- **Warm-Up (5):** *Guess the Trend.* Three weeks of numbers; predict week four.
- **Mini-Lesson (10):** A tracker is a table, not an art project: date, three numbers, one note.
  The note is where you record *why* — future you will not remember.
- **Work (35):** Build the tracker on paper or in a spreadsheet. Run the first check-in with the
  four questions: *Where are we? What changed? Why? What will we do?*
- **Close (10):** Each Lead states the team's headline number.
- **Artifact:** Tracker with entries + first check-in notes.

### S3 · SHOW — Notice what the numbers say

- **Warm-Up (5):** *Up, Down, Flat.* Read three simple charts out loud.
- **Mini-Lesson (10):** Three moves when a number is bad: change the thing, change the price,
  change who you are selling to. One at a time, or you will not know what worked.
- **Work (35):** Update the tracker with new data, then write a **decision card**: what we saw,
  what we decided, what we expect, when we will check.
- **Close (10):** Decision cards pinned with their check dates.
- **Artifact:** Decision card.

### S4 · CHANGE — Check and own it

- **Warm-Up (5):** *Predictions Revisited.*
- **Mini-Lesson (10):** Owning it: "I decided that, here is what happened, here is what I
  learned." The Guide models it with a real decision of their own that went badly.
- **Work (35):** Second check-in. Compare the decision card to what actually happened. Write a
  short **CEO note**.
- **Close (10):** Badge check: **Young CEO**, **Number Cruncher**, **Keep Building**.
- **Artifact:** CEO note + updated tracker.

## 🚀 Young Founders (11–13) — 4 × 75 min

### S1 · NOTICE — Goals with a number and a date

- **Warm-Up (8):** *Is That a Goal?* "Do well at Market Day" vs. "Sell 40 units by 3 pm on
  May 12." Sort ten.
- **Mini-Lesson (12):** A goal has a number and a date. Then three measures spanning money,
  customer, and work — each defined so two people would count it identically.
- **Work (42):** Write a four-week goal and pick the three measures. Ambiguous definitions get
  sent back: "customers" means what, precisely?
- **Close (13):** Goals and definitions on the wall through Course 9.
- **Artifact:** Goal + three defined measures.

### S2 · MAKE — Dashboard and rhythm

- **Warm-Up (8):** *One Screen.* Sketch everything you need to know about your venture on one
  index card.
- **Mini-Lesson (12):** A dashboard is one page, updated on a fixed day, that answers "are we on
  track?" in ten seconds. Then the cadence: stand-up, weekly review, retro.
- **Work (42):** Build the dashboard, populate with real data to date, and run weekly review one
  against the goal.
- **Close (13):** Screens shown side by side; steal one good idea from another team.
- **Artifact:** Dashboard + weekly review one.

### S3 · SHOW — A decision the data caused

- **Warm-Up (8):** *Data Says No.* You love your product; nobody bought twice. What now?
- **Mini-Lesson (12):** A decision record: context, options considered, decision, expected
  outcome, review date, owner. Written *before* you find out.
- **Work (42):** Make one substantive decision from a number, record it, act on it, and present it
  to another team for challenge.
- **Close (13):** Any decision that could not survive challenge is reopened, not defended.
- **Artifact:** Decision record, in progress.

### S4 · CHANGE — Review, adjust, plan

- **Warm-Up (8):** *Forecast vs. Actual.*
- **Mini-Lesson (12):** Weekly review two: did the decision do what you expected? Then the Course
  9 sprint plan — goal, measures, roles, cadence, budget.
- **Work (42):** Run the review, check the decision against reality, and write the sprint plan.
- **Close (13):** Badge check: **Young CEO**, **Number Cruncher**, **Keep Building**, **Future
  Leader**.
- **Artifact:** Weekly review two + Course 9 sprint plan.

## 👑 Founder CEOs (14–17) — 4 × 90 min

### S1 · NOTICE — What actually matters

- **Warm-Up (10):** *Vanity vs. Value.* Sort ten metrics into ones that flatter and ones that
  inform. Followers vs. repeat purchases; views vs. conversion.
- **Mini-Lesson (15):** One north-star metric plus three to five supporting measures across
  money, customer, and operations. Leading vs. lagging indicators — you need both, and only
  leading ones let you act in time.
- **Work (50):** Choose the north star with a written justification. Define every metric so two
  people would count it identically. Set targets for the Course 9 sprint.
- **Close (15):** Defend your north star to a challenging peer team.
- **Artifact:** Metric definitions + targets + justification.

### S2 · MAKE — Dashboard and cadence

- **Warm-Up (10):** *One Screen.*
- **Mini-Lesson (15):** One screen, fixed update day, ten-second read. The cadence: daily
  stand-up, weekly review, sprint retro — each with a different question.
- **Work (50):** Build the dashboard in a spreadsheet, linked to the Course 3 ledger so money
  numbers are never retyped. Populate with real data. Run weekly review one against targets.
- **Close (15):** Steal one good idea from another team's screen.
- **Artifact:** Live dashboard + weekly review one.

### S3 · SHOW — Add, cut, or keep

- **Warm-Up (10):** *Kill a Feature.* Name the part of your product you would defend hardest.
  Then the room argues for cutting it.
- **Mini-Lesson (15):** Trade-offs and opportunity cost — every yes is a no to something else.
  Then the decision record, written before the outcome is known.
- **Work (50):** Make one substantive add/cut/keep decision on evidence, record it, and
  communicate it to the people it affects using the Course 6 handover format. Cross-team review
  panel challenges two decisions per team.
- **Close (15):** Reopen anything that could not survive challenge.
- **Artifact:** Two decision records + evidence.

### S4 · CHANGE — Accountability and the sprint plan

- **Warm-Up (10):** *Own It.* Each learner states one thing that went wrong that was theirs.
- **Mini-Lesson (15):** Accountability without blame: the outcome is mine, the cause is the
  system, the fix is specific. Then the sprint plan, including the **stop-rule** — the condition
  under which you would halt.
- **Work (50):** Write the Course 9 operating plan combining the Course 6 operating agreement, the
  Course 3 cash plan, and this dashboard. The stop-rule goes in writing now, before there is
  emotional investment in continuing.
- **Close (15):** Badge check: **Young CEO**, **Number Cruncher**, **Keep Building**, **Future
  Leader**.
- **Artifact:** Course 9 operating plan with stop-rule.
- **Mentor touchpoint:** A mentor reviews two dashboards and asks: "what would you do tomorrow if
  this number halved?"

## Evidence & badge criteria

| Badge | Criteria | Points |
|---|---|---|
| **Young CEO** (core) | Tracked measures across two review cycles and made one decision the data caused | 25 |
| **Number Cruncher** | A correct, self-checked calculation that changed what the team did | 25 |
| **Keep Building** | Reported a bad number honestly and acted on it rather than hiding or explaining it away | 25 |
| **Future Leader** | Ran a review the team found useful, without the Guide | 25 |

Session completion: 10 Little Points each (40 for the course).

**Rubric — running it:**

| | Emerging | Secure | Strong |
|---|---|---|---|
| Measures | Counts whatever is easy | Three defined, relevant measures | North star justified; leading and lagging both present |
| Honesty | Reports only good news | Reports all numbers | Volunteers the bad number first, unprompted |
| Decision | Decides on preference | Traceable to a number | States expected outcome and review date in advance |
| Ownership | Blames circumstances | Accepts the outcome | Owns it, diagnoses the cause, changes the system |

## Differentiation & access

- **Paper tracking is fully valid at every tier.** A wall chart updated by hand often beats a
  spreadsheet nobody opens.
- **Spreadsheet barriers:** Pair with a template pre-built with formulas. The assessed skill is
  interpretation, not formula authoring.
- **Anxiety around bad numbers:** Frame it explicitly and repeatedly — a bad number found early is
  a good outcome. Award **Keep Building** publicly for honest bad news, every time.
- **Extension (Builders):** Track a fourth measure and argue whether it earned its place.
- **Extension (Founders and CEOs):** Forecast each week and compare predicted to actual. The gap
  is more instructive than either number.

## Guide watch-outs

- **Metric theatre.** Beautiful dashboards nobody uses. Ask every week: "what did this number make
  you do?" If the answer is nothing, the metric is wrong.
- **Retyping numbers.** Link or copy money numbers once from the Course 3 ledger. Divergent
  versions of the truth cause pointless arguments.
- **The Guide making the decision.** Your instinct will be to steer them off a bad call. Let small
  bad calls happen; that is the entire point of Session 4.
- **Counting only good things.** Teams track sales and never cost or returns. Require one measure
  the team would prefer not to see.
- **Review as a report to the adult.** It is a meeting the team runs for itself. Sit outside the
  circle.
- **Stop-rules written after emotional investment.** Write it in Session 4. A stop-rule invented
  mid-sprint is never used.

## Materials

Tally charts and big paper · tracker templates (paper and spreadsheet) · decision card stock ·
review question cards · spreadsheet access (Founders, CEOs) · the Course 3 ledger · wall space
for goals and decision cards.

## Home extension — 10 minutes

**🌱 Sprouts:** *Count Something.* Choose one thing at home to count for three days. Draw the
chart.

**🔨 Builders:** *Family Numbers.* Pick three numbers that would tell you how your week went.
Track them for a week.

**🚀 Young Founders:** *Goal With a Date.* Set one goal with a number and a date for something
outside business. Tell someone at home.

**👑 Founder CEOs:** *Your Own Dashboard.* Choose three measures for something you care about —
training, practice, sleep. Track for a week and make one change from what you see.

## Family note (after Session 4)

> Your child has moved from making things to running things. They chose what to measure, looked at
> numbers they did not always like, and made a decision because of what they saw.
>
> **Ask at dinner:** *"What number are you watching, and what is it telling you to do?"*
