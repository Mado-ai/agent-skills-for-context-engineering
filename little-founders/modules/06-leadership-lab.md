# Course 6 — Leadership Lab

**Icon:** People · **Colour:** Sky · **Core badge:** Future Leader · **Sessions:** 4
**Unlocks at:** Courses 1–5 complete **and** 250 Little Points · **Français:** Labo du Leadership

> Leaders make the people around them stronger.

## Big idea

Leadership is not being in charge. It is making sure the work gets done, the team stays whole,
and everyone knows what they are doing — including when people disagree. It is a set of
behaviours that can be practised, not a personality you either have or do not.

## Essential questions

- What does a team need from a leader that it cannot give itself?
- What do I do when two people I like want opposite things?
- How do I ask for help without feeling small?

## Objectives by tier

| | A learner can… |
|---|---|
| 🌱 **Little Sprouts** | Take a job on a team, do it, say kind and true things, and let someone else have a turn |
| 🔨 **Junior Builders** | Hold a defined role, run a 5-minute stand-up, and help write and use a team agreement |
| 🚀 **Young Founders** | Lead a work block, rotate leadership fairly, and hold a real conversation about a real friction |
| 👑 **Founder CEOs** | Delegate with a clear handover, run a decision when the team disagrees, give hard feedback kindly, and name their own weakness |

## Vocabulary

| Term | Plain meaning | From tier |
|---|---|---|
| Team | People working on the same thing | Sprouts |
| Job / role | The part that is yours | Sprouts |
| Turn-taking | Everyone gets their go | Sprouts |
| Team agreement | Rules we wrote together | Builders |
| Stand-up | A short daily check-in | Builders |
| Rotation | Everyone leads something | Founders |
| Delegation | Giving work away properly | CEOs |
| Decision rights | Who gets to decide what | CEOs |
| Accountability | Being the one it comes back to | CEOs |

## 🌱 Little Sprouts (5–7) — 4 × 45 min

### S1 · NOTICE — Everybody has a job

- **Warm-Up (5):** *Human Machine.* The group builds a machine where each learner is a moving
  part. Stop when one part quits — what happened?
- **Mini-Lesson (7):** Teams work when everyone knows their job. Four job cards: **Maker**,
  **Teller**, **Timekeeper**, **Tidier**.
- **Work (25):** **Job Cards.** Teams of four build a paper tower with assigned cards, then swap
  all cards and build again. Which round felt better?
- **Close (8):** Which job would you like next time?
- **Artifact:** Job card held + tower photo.

### S2 · MAKE — Kind and true

- **Warm-Up (5):** *Compliment Ball.* Say one true good thing about the catcher's *work* — not
  their appearance.
- **Mini-Lesson (7):** "Nice" is kind. "You kept trying when the tower fell" is kind *and* true.
- **Work (25):** **Star Notes** for every teammate, naming one true thing they did. Delivered and
  read aloud.
- **Close (8):** Notes into the workbook. Nobody leaves with fewer than three.
- **Artifact:** Star notes given and received.

### S3 · SHOW — Turn-taking under pressure

- **Warm-Up (5):** *One Pencil.* Four learners, one pencil, one drawing to finish.
- **Mini-Lesson (7):** When everyone wants the same thing: take turns, share, or swap. Three hand
  signals.
- **Work (25):** **The Squeeze.** Complete a build with deliberately scarce materials — one pair
  of scissors, three pieces of tape. Helpers observe and record strategies without intervening
  unless someone is upset.
- **Close (8):** Helpers report what they saw. Name the good moves.
- **Artifact:** Observed strategy noted by a Helper.

### S4 · CHANGE — Our team rules

- **Warm-Up (5):** *Rules That Work.* Which class rules actually help?
- **Mini-Lesson (7):** Teams that make their own rules follow them.
- **Work (25):** Four picture rules on a poster, signed with a handprint or name.
- **Close (8):** Badge check: **Future Leader**, **Team Player**, **Helping Hand**.
- **Artifact:** Signed team rules poster.

## 🔨 Junior Builders (8–10) — 4 × 60 min

### S1 · NOTICE — Roles that mean something

- **Warm-Up (5):** *Who Did What?* Recall the last team task; list who did what. The gaps are the
  lesson.
- **Mini-Lesson (10):** Four roles with real duties — **Lead** (holds the goal, unblocks),
  **Maker** (builds), **Money** (tracks spend), **Voice** (talks to customers). Every role has
  one thing only they may decide.
- **Work (35):** Assign roles by discussion, not by who shouts. Each learner writes a role card:
  *what I decide · what I do each session · what I need from others.* Run one 20-minute task with
  roles live.
- **Close (10):** Anyone whose card was wrong in practice rewrites one line.
- **Artifact:** Role card + observed role in action.

### S2 · MAKE — Stand-ups and the agreement

- **Warm-Up (5):** *60-Second Update.* What I did, what I'm doing, what's in my way.
- **Mini-Lesson (10):** Five minutes, standing, no problem-solving — blockers get named and taken
  away for later. Then the team agreement: how we decide, how we disagree, what happens when
  someone does not do their job, how we split the money.
- **Work (35):** Run a real stand-up (Lead facilitates, Guide times). Draft the agreement in the
  team's own words, including a consequence they choose themselves.
- **Close (10):** Agreements read aloud; every member signs.
- **Artifact:** Signed team agreement + first stand-up run.

### S3 · SHOW — Conflict, on purpose

- **Warm-Up (5):** *Two Truths.* Argue opposite sides of a trivial question for 60 seconds, then
  swap sides.
- **Mini-Lesson (10):** Disagreements about *the work* are healthy; disagreements about *people*
  are not. Three steps: say what you see, say what you need, ask what they think.
- **Work (35):** **Scenario Lab** — a member who never finishes, a price two people cannot agree
  on, someone who took over everything. Role-play the conversation, then name one real friction
  in your own team and hold that conversation with the Guide nearby.
- **Close (10):** What was harder than expected?
- **Artifact:** Scenario notes + one real conversation held.

### S4 · CHANGE — Lead a block

- **Warm-Up (5):** *Handover.* Explain a task in 30 seconds so a partner could do it.
- **Mini-Lesson (10):** Rotating leadership — everyone leads something before Course 9.
- **Work (35):** Each team plans and runs a 10-minute work block led by someone who has not led.
  The Guide steps fully back and takes notes.
- **Close (10):** Badge check: **Future Leader**, **Team Player**, **Helping Hand**. Rotation
  schedule for Course 9 agreed.
- **Artifact:** Led a block + peer feedback on it.

## 🚀 Young Founders (11–13) — 4 × 75 min

### S1 · NOTICE — What leaders actually do

- **Warm-Up (8):** *Best and Worst.* Describe the best and worst team leader you have
  experienced. Extract behaviours, not personalities.
- **Mini-Lesson (12):** Four behaviours that carry most of leadership: set a clear goal, remove
  blockers, close the loop, take the blame and share the credit.
- **Work (42):** Self-assess against the four with evidence from Courses 1–5. Peers add one
  observation each. Write one thing you will do differently this course.
- **Close (13):** Read your commitment aloud.
- **Artifact:** Self-assessment + written commitment.

### S2 · MAKE — Rotation and the agreement

- **Warm-Up (8):** *60-Second Update.*
- **Mini-Lesson (12):** A rotation schedule that is fair rather than convenient — everyone leads,
  including the quiet ones and the ones who did badly last time. Then the team agreement, with
  the uncomfortable line: what happens when someone does not deliver?
- **Work (42):** Write the rotation schedule through Course 9. Draft and sign the team agreement.
  Run a stand-up led by the first person on the rotation.
- **Close (13):** Each team reads the consequence line they wrote for themselves.
- **Artifact:** Rotation schedule + signed agreement.

### S3 · SHOW — The real conversation

- **Warm-Up (8):** *Feedback Sandwich Is Dead.* Rewrite three sandwich-style feedbacks as direct
  ones that stay kind.
- **Mini-Lesson (12):** Situation → behaviour → impact → request. Then receiving: listen, ask one
  clarifying question, thank, decide later.
- **Work (42):** Structured peer feedback: every learner gives and receives from two teammates,
  named in advance. The Guide circulates and coaches phrasing in the moment. Then each team names
  and addresses one real friction.
- **Close (13):** One thing you heard that you did not enjoy but think is true.
- **Artifact:** Feedback log + one friction addressed.

### S4 · CHANGE — Lead a full session block

- **Warm-Up (8):** *Failure Modes.* Predict the three ways your team will fail during Course 9.
- **Mini-Lesson (12):** An operating rhythm: stand-up, mid-point check, end retro. Plus an
  escalation rule for when the team cannot resolve something itself.
- **Work (42):** Each team runs a 20-minute block led by a rotation member, with the Guide fully
  out. Then write the operating agreement for Course 9.
- **Close (13):** Badge check: **Future Leader**, **Team Player**, **Helping Hand**, **Deal
  Maker**.
- **Artifact:** Led a block + signed operating agreement.

## 👑 Founder CEOs (14–17) — 4 × 90 min

### S1 · NOTICE — Leadership as behaviour

- **Warm-Up (10):** *Best and Worst*, extracting behaviours.
- **Mini-Lesson (15):** The four behaviours, then styles — directive, coaching, consensus — and
  when each fits. Consensus on everything is how teams stall.
- **Work (50):** Self-assessment with evidence. Peers add observations. Then each learner writes
  their leadership weakness in one sentence and one thing they will do differently.
- **Close (15):** Weaknesses read aloud voluntarily. The Guide goes first, honestly — this sets
  the ceiling for the room.
- **Artifact:** Self-assessment + named weakness + commitment.

### S2 · MAKE — Delegation and decision rights

- **Warm-Up (10):** *Bad Handover.* The Guide delegates a task terribly; the room lists what was
  missing.
- **Mini-Lesson (15):** A handover contains outcome, constraints, deadline, decision rights,
  check-in point. Then decision rights: which decisions are consent-based ("no strong objection")
  and which are owner-decided.
- **Work (50):** Build a **decision map** — the next 15 decisions of the venture, each with an
  owner and a type. Then delegate three real tasks with full written handovers.
- **Close (15):** Read one handover; the room checks all five parts are present.
- **Artifact:** Decision map + three written handovers.

### S3 · SHOW — Hard conversations

- **Warm-Up (10):** *Feedback Sandwich Is Dead.*
- **Mini-Lesson (15):** Situation → behaviour → impact → request, practised on a low-stakes
  example first. Then receiving without defending.
- **Work (50):** Structured feedback round with two named partners each, Guide coaching phrasing
  live. Then a retrospective on Course 5 pitch week: keep, drop, try.
- **Close (15):** One thing you heard that you did not enjoy but think is true.
- **Artifact:** Feedback given and received, logged + retro output.

### S4 · CHANGE — Operating rhythm for Course 9

- **Warm-Up (10):** *Failure Modes.*
- **Mini-Lesson (15):** Daily stand-up, mid-point check, end-of-sprint retro — each answering a
  different question. Plus the escalation rule.
- **Work (50):** Write the **operating agreement** for Course 9: roles, rotation, stand-up time,
  decision map, split rule from Course 3, conflict process, escalation rule. Then a premortem —
  assume it failed, write why, fix the two most likely causes now.
- **Close (15):** Badge check: **Future Leader**, **Team Player**, **Helping Hand**, **Deal
  Maker** where a negotiated agreement resolved a real conflict.
- **Artifact:** Signed operating agreement + premortem.

## Evidence & badge criteria

| Badge | Criteria | Points |
|---|---|---|
| **Future Leader** (core) | Led a real piece of work, and teammates can describe what that leadership did for them | 25 |
| **Team Player** | Held a role reliably and completed what they took on, or renegotiated it openly | 25 |
| **Helping Hand** | Gave specific, useful feedback or unblocked a teammate on 10 occasions (cumulative) | 25 |
| **Deal Maker** | Resolved a disagreement with an arrangement both sides called fair | 25 |

Session completion: 10 Little Points each (40 for the course).

**Rubric — leadership:**

| | Emerging | Secure | Strong |
|---|---|---|---|
| Reliability | Work does not arrive | Does their part | Does their part and flags risk early |
| Clarity | Others are unsure what to do | Communicates the task | Handovers others can act on without asking |
| Repair | Avoids conflict or escalates it | Raises issues calmly | Raises, resolves, and the relationship survives |
| Self-knowledge | Cannot name a weakness | Names one | Names one, acts on it, asks for feedback on the change |

## Differentiation & access

- **Quiet learners are not "not leaders."** All four behaviours can be done quietly. Make sure
  the rotation reaches learners who will not volunteer.
- **Learners who dominate:** Give them Timekeeper or Money, where the job is to serve someone
  else's work. Name the pattern privately, never publicly.
- **Learners for whom conflict has been unsafe:** Scenario Lab role-play is opt-in; observing and
  writing the analysis carries equal credit.
- **Neurodivergent learners:** Write the stand-up format on a card and keep it visible. Explicit
  structure helps everyone and is essential for some.
- **Extension:** Lead a team that is not your own for one session, then compare.

## Guide watch-outs

- **Leadership as a popularity contest.** Never elect leaders by vote at Junior Builders — you
  will select for confidence and social status. Assign or rotate.
- **The Guide who cannot step back.** Session 4 fails entirely if you keep talking. Sit down.
  Take notes. Let the block go slightly wrong.
- **Agreements written to please the adult.** If every agreement says "be kind, try hard," push
  for the uncomfortable line: *what happens when someone does not do their part?*
- **Frictionless teams.** A team with no conflict usually has one person doing everything, or
  nobody caring. Look for it in Session 3.
- **Feedback that becomes a pile-on.** Two named partners, using the frame. Never open-floor
  criticism of an individual.
- **Real conflicts that need an adult.** Anything involving exclusion, cruelty, or safeguarding is
  not a learning scenario. Stop the exercise and follow the setting's policy.

## Home extension — 10 minutes

**🌱 Sprouts:** *Jobs at Home.* Name everyone's job in your household. What happens when one is
not done?

**🔨 Builders:** *Run the Stand-up.* A 5-minute family stand-up: what everyone did, is doing, and
needs help with.

**🚀 Young Founders:** *The Fair Rotation.* Who at home always does the same job? Propose one swap
for a week.

**👑 Founder CEOs:** *Ask for Feedback.* Ask one adult who knows you well: "what is one thing I do
that makes teamwork harder?" Listen. Do not defend. Write it down.
