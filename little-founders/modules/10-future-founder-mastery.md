# Course 10 — Future Founder Mastery

**Icon:** Trophy · **Colour:** Sunshine · **Core badge:** Future Founder · **Sessions:** 4
**Prerequisites:** Course 9 · **Awards the tier certification** · **Français:** Fondateur de Demain

> Your story, your evidence, your next step.

## Big idea

The program ends with the learner, not the venture. What did you become good at? What did you find
out about yourself? What are you doing next — and what is the smallest first step?

**Milestone:** Demo Day closes Session 4, and the tier certification is awarded there.

## Essential questions

- What can I do now that I could not do in Course 1?
- What is my evidence?
- What am I doing next, starting this week?

## Objectives by tier

| | A learner can… | Certification |
|---|---|---|
| 🌱 **Little Sprouts** | Show three things they made, say what they learned, name one thing to try next | **Junior Founder** |
| 🔨 **Junior Builders** | Assemble a portfolio, give a 3-minute Demo Day talk, and set a next goal with a first step | **Certified Builder** |
| 🚀 **Young Founders** | Run a retrospective, present with evidence, and write a next-term plan with milestones | **Certified Young Founder** |
| 👑 **Founder CEOs** | Run a full retrospective, present to a public audience, make a reasoned continue/pivot/stop decision, and write a 90-day plan | **Certified Young Entrepreneur** |

## Vocabulary

| Term | Plain meaning | From tier |
|---|---|---|
| Learning | Something I can do now that I could not before | Sprouts |
| Proud | The feeling when you made something hard | Sprouts |
| Portfolio | The collection that proves what I did | Builders |
| Goal | What I want next, with a date | Builders |
| Retrospective | Looking back to learn on purpose | Founders |
| Milestone | A checkpoint on the way to the goal | Founders |
| Pivot | Changing direction, keeping what you learned | CEOs |
| 90-day plan | The next three months, in steps | CEOs |

## 🌱 Little Sprouts (5–7) — 4 × 45 min

### S1 · NOTICE — My best bits

- **Warm-Up (5):** *Remember When.* Guide holds up artifacts from earlier courses; learners call
  out the memory.
- **Mini-Lesson (7):** We are going to show everyone what we did this year.
- **Work (25):** **My Three Things.** Choose three pieces from your folder and say why each
  matters. Helpers scribe the reason onto a label.
- **Close (8):** One learner shows their three things to the room.
- **Artifact:** Three chosen pieces with scribed reasons.

### S2 · MAKE — My founder book

- **Warm-Up (5):** *Then and Now.* Compare your Course 1 drawing to your Course 9 shop.
- **Mini-Lesson (7):** A book about you: what I made, what I learned, what I want next.
- **Work (25):** Build a four-page founder book — cover with name and drawing, my idea, my best
  bit, next time I will. Brand colours and stickers.
- **Close (8):** Books shown to a partner.
- **Artifact:** Founder book.

### S3 · SHOW — Practise the showing

- **Warm-Up (5):** *Big Voice Again.*
- **Mini-Lesson (7):** At Demo Day you show your book and say three sentences: *I made… I
  learned… Next I want to…*
- **Work (25):** Practise in trios with Helpers, three rounds. Set up the display table.
- **Close (8):** Invitations home; the room is decorated.
- **Artifact:** Rehearsed three sentences.

### S4 · CHANGE + 🏆 DEMO DAY

- **Warm-Up (5):** *Shake It Out.*
- **Mini-Lesson (7):** Everyone gets a turn. Everyone claps for everyone.
- **Work (25):** **Demo Day.** Families visit. Each learner shows their book and says their three
  sentences at their own table. Photo booth open with the brand props.
- **Close (8):** **Junior Founder** certificates and the **Future Founder** badge. Group photo
  under the *Dream · Plan · Build · Grow* signpost.
- **Artifact:** Presented at Demo Day.

## 🔨 Junior Builders (8–10) — 4 × 60 min

### S1 · NOTICE — Retrospective

- **Warm-Up (5):** *Timeline.* Lay out the ten courses; mark the hardest and best moments with
  dots.
- **Mini-Lesson (10):** A retrospective asks three questions: what worked, what did not, what
  would we do differently. About us, not just the product.
- **Work (35):** Team retro on sticky notes across all ten courses, then individually: *three
  things I can do now that I could not in September, with evidence for each.* Evidence must be an
  artifact, not a feeling.
- **Close (10):** Read one "I can now…" statement each.
- **Artifact:** Team retro + three evidenced growth statements.

### S2 · MAKE — The portfolio

- **Warm-Up (5):** *Curate.* Given eight pieces, which four tell the best story?
- **Mini-Lesson (10):** A portfolio is chosen, not collected. Six pieces, each with one line: what
  it is, and what it shows about me. Include at least one thing that failed.
- **Work (35):** Assemble: problem sheet (C1), plan (C2), cost sheet and price defence (C3),
  poster (C4), pitch script or recording (C5), Startup Report (C9), plus one free choice. Write
  the captions.
- **Close (10):** Swap portfolios; write one thing you noticed about the other person's growth.
- **Artifact:** Curated portfolio with captions.

### S3 · SHOW — Demo Day talk

- **Warm-Up (5):** *Hook Again* — now about you rather than a product.
- **Mini-Lesson (10):** The 3-minute journey talk: where I started, what I built, what went wrong,
  what I learned, what is next. The "what went wrong" section is required and is usually the part
  the audience remembers.
- **Work (35):** Draft and rehearse in threes with the feedback frame. Two timed rounds. Set up
  the display.
- **Close (10):** One volunteer performs to the whole room.
- **Artifact:** Rehearsed 3-minute talk.

### S4 · CHANGE + 🏆 DEMO DAY

- **Mini-Lesson (10):** The next-step plan: goal, first action, date, who can help.
- **Work (35):** **Demo Day.** Families, staff, and partners attend. Each learner gives their talk;
  stalls and portfolios on display; photo booth open.
- **Close (10):** Next-step plans written and read to one adult. **Certified Builder** certificates
  and the **Future Founder** badge awarded.
- **Artifact:** Demo Day talk + next-step plan.

## 🚀 Young Founders (11–13) — 4 × 75 min

### S1 · NOTICE — Retro against the target

- **Warm-Up (8):** *Sealed Envelope.* Reread the target you wrote in Course 9 Session 1 and the
  goal from Course 7.
- **Mini-Lesson (12):** Keep / drop / try, then a causal pass — for each outcome, which decision
  caused it?
- **Work (42):** Team retro, then an individual self-assessment against the program outcomes with
  evidence for each. Where evidence is missing, say so rather than claiming it.
- **Close (13):** State your weakest area and what you would do about it.
- **Artifact:** Retro + evidenced self-assessment.

### S2 · MAKE — Portfolio and next-term plan

- **Warm-Up (8):** *Curate.*
- **Mini-Lesson (12):** Six pieces, captioned, one of them a failure. Then the next-term plan: one
  goal, three milestones with dates, and the first action — doable within seven days.
- **Work (42):** Assemble and caption the portfolio. Write the plan. Peer challenge in pairs:
  "what is the weakest assumption in this plan?"
- **Close (13):** Read the first action you will take this week.
- **Artifact:** Portfolio + next-term plan.

### S3 · SHOW — Demo Day preparation

- **Warm-Up (8):** *Two Versions.* Your story in 60 seconds, then in 10.
- **Mini-Lesson (12):** The 4-minute talk: the venture in one line, the target vs. what happened,
  the thing that went wrong, what you learned, what is next. Then slides — max 5,
  brand-consistent, six words on screen.
- **Work (42):** Build slides, rehearse twice, self-assess against the Course 5 rubric, revise.
- **Close (13):** Peer rehearsal panel; one strength and one fix each.
- **Artifact:** Final deck + rehearsed talk.

### S4 · CHANGE + 🏆 DEMO DAY

- **Mini-Lesson (12):** Demo Day conduct: know your slot, listen to every other talk, follow up
  with anyone who responds within 48 hours.
- **Work (42):** **Demo Day.** Families, mentors, and partners attend. Four-minute talks with
  questions. Portfolios displayed. Photo booth open.
- **Close (13):** **Certified Young Founder** certificates and the **Future Founder** badge.
  Follow-up commitments written before anyone leaves.
- **Artifact:** Demo Day talk + follow-up commitments.

## 👑 Founder CEOs (14–17) — 4 × 90 min

### S1 · NOTICE — Full retrospective and self-assessment

- **Warm-Up (10):** *Sealed Envelope.* Reread the goals and predictions from Courses 1, 7, and 9.
- **Mini-Lesson (15):** Keep / drop / try, then the causal pass — for each outcome, what decision
  caused it, and what would you have needed to know to decide differently?
- **Work (50):** Team retro, then individual self-assessment against all ten program outcomes with
  evidence. Then a skills inventory: strongest, weakest, most surprising.
- **Close (15):** State your weakest area out loud and what you would do about it.
- **Artifact:** Retro + evidenced self-assessment.

### S2 · MAKE — The decision and the 90-day plan

- **Warm-Up (10):** *Sunk Cost.* Two scenarios where the honest answer is to stop.
- **Mini-Lesson (15):** Continue, pivot, or stop — is there demand, can the economics work, do you
  want to? Any of the three is a success if reasoned. Then the 90-day plan: one goal, three
  milestones, a weekly first action, the resources and people needed, and what would make you
  reconsider.
- **Work (50):** Write the decision with reasoning against Course 9 evidence, then the plan. Peer
  challenge: "what is the weakest assumption here?"
- **Close (15):** Read the first action you will take this week — doable within seven days.
- **Artifact:** Reasoned decision + 90-day plan.

### S3 · SHOW — Demo Day preparation

- **Warm-Up (10):** *Two Versions.*
- **Mini-Lesson (15):** The 5-minute founder talk: the venture in one line, the number that
  matters, the thing that went wrong, what you learned, the decision, the ask. End with a real ask
  of the room — a contact, advice, a customer, a collaborator.
- **Work (50):** Build slides (max 6, brand-consistent), rehearse twice on video, self-assess
  against the Course 5 rubric, revise. Finalise the portfolio: canvas, unit economics, pitch
  video, Venture Report, community plan, self-assessment, 90-day plan.
- **Close (15):** Peer rehearsal panel; one strength and one fix each.
- **Artifact:** Final deck + rehearsed talk + complete portfolio.

### S4 · CHANGE + 🏆 DEMO DAY

- **Warm-Up (10):** *The Ask*, rehearsed once more — it is the line people forget.
- **Mini-Lesson (15):** Demo Day conduct and the 48-hour follow-up rule.
- **Work (50):** **Demo Day.** Public audience — families, mentors, partners, local business
  people. Five-minute talks with Q&A. Portfolios displayed. Photo booth open.
- **Close (15):** **Certified Young Entrepreneur** certificates and the **Future Founder** badge.
  Alumni invitation — returning as a Mentor or Helper for the next cohort. Follow-up commitments
  written before anyone leaves.
- **Artifact:** Demo Day talk + follow-up commitments.

## Demo Day — running it

**Format (90–120 minutes)**

| Time | What |
|---|---|
| 0:00 | Doors, photo booth open, portfolios on display |
| 0:15 | Welcome — one Guide, three minutes maximum |
| 0:20 | Talks, in tier order, youngest first |
| 1:10 | Break, browse portfolios, families talk to learners |
| 1:25 | Certification and badge ceremony |
| 1:45 | Group photo under the signpost; close |

**Rules**

- Every learner presents. All Course 5 access routes apply in full.
- No ranking, no winner, no prizes for best business. Certifications are criterion-based.
- Adults may ask questions; adults may not answer for a learner.
- Photography only against the consent list; a marked no-photo zone is available.
- The Guide's welcome does not explain the program. Let the work do it.

**Invitations:** families, Course 8 partners, mentors, staff, and the next cohort's learners — the
last group matters most for the program's culture.

## Certifications

Each tier awards its own certification at Demo Day. It is the completion badge for that tier and
carries 50 Little Points.

| Tier | Certification | Criteria |
|---|---|---|
| 🌱 Little Sprouts | **Junior Founder** | All ten courses complete; founder book held; presented at Demo Day |
| 🔨 Junior Builders | **Certified Builder** | All ten courses; portfolio of six pieces including one failure; 3-minute talk |
| 🚀 Young Founders | **Certified Young Founder** | All ten courses; portfolio; validated venture reported against target; 4-minute talk |
| 👑 Founder CEOs | **Certified Young Entrepreneur** | All ten courses; full portfolio; reasoned continue/pivot/stop decision; 90-day plan; 5-minute talk with Q&A |

A learner who joined late or missed courses receives **Future Founder** on the evidence they hold;
the certification requires all ten courses and may be completed in a later cohort. Say this
warmly, not as a penalty.

## Evidence & badge criteria

| Badge | Criteria | Points |
|---|---|---|
| **Future Founder** (core) | Presented their journey at Demo Day with evidence and named a next step | 25 |
| **Tier certification** | See the table above | 50 |
| **Show & Tell** | Fifth presentation completed (usually here) | 25 |
| **Keep Building** | Portfolio includes a failure and what was learned from it | 25 |
| **Streak Star** | Attended and contributed across a sustained run | 25 |

Session completion: 10 Little Points each (40 for the course). Demo Day: 50 points.

**Rubric — mastery:**

| | Emerging | Secure | Strong |
|---|---|---|---|
| Self-knowledge | "It was good" | Names what they learned | Names it, evidences it, and names what they are still bad at |
| Evidence | Assertions | Portfolio supports the claims | Every claim traceable to an artifact, including failures |
| Communication | Reads a script | Clear, structured, on time | Holds the room, handles questions, makes a real ask |
| Forward step | No plan | A goal | A goal, a first action within seven days, and a named person to help |

## Differentiation & access

- **All Course 5 access routes apply** — pre-recorded, signed, partner-read, one-person audience.
  Every route earns full **Future Founder** and full certification credit.
- **Portfolios for non-writers:** photographs, recordings, and scribed captions throughout.
- **Bilingual cohorts:** present in either language, with a one-line summary in the other so the
  whole room can follow.
- **Families who cannot attend:** record the talk and send it. Arrange a proxy audience member so
  no learner presents to an empty chair.
- **Extension:** Present at a wider event — a school board meeting, a local business network, the
  next cohort's launch.

## Guide watch-outs

- **Turning Demo Day into a competition.** The moment there is a winner, the quiet learner who grew
  most disappears. Hold this line even when a well-meaning sponsor offers a prize — redirect them
  to fund materials or mentoring instead.
- **Polishing away the failures.** A portfolio with no failures is a weaker portfolio and is marked
  down against the rubric. Say this in Session 2, before curation.
- **The plan nobody does.** A plan whose first action cannot start this week is a wish. Push until
  the first action is small and dated.
- **Parents answering questions.** Brief families in the invitation: the young people answer.
- **The flat feeling afterwards.** Common and normal. Announce the alumni route — Helper, Mentor,
  next cohort — at Demo Day so the energy has somewhere to go.
- **Guides who talk too long at the ceremony.** Three minutes. Set a timer and give it to a
  learner.

## Materials

Portfolio folders or boxes · caption labels · display tables and stands · certificates printed per
tier (name + certification) · badge stickers and pin badges · photo booth props and frame ·
projector and laptop (Founders, CEOs) · timer · camera · alumni invitation slips · next-step and
90-day plan templates.

## Home extension — 10 minutes

**🌱 Sprouts:** *Show Your Book.* Read your founder book to someone at home. Ask them their
favourite page.

**🔨 Builders:** *The First Step.* Tell someone at home your next goal and first action. Ask them to
check in with you in two weeks.

**🚀 Young Founders:** *Three Milestones.* Explain your next-term plan and its three milestones.
Ask which one they think will slip.

**👑 Founder CEOs:** *Send the Follow-Up.* Contact one person who responded to your Demo Day ask,
within 48 hours, with a specific request.

## Family note (after Demo Day)

> Your child has finished their year at Little Founders. Over ten courses they found a problem,
> built something, priced it, marketed it, pitched it, led a team, ran the numbers, worked with
> their community, sold to real customers, and stood up in front of a room to tell the truth about
> how it went.
>
> Their certificate says **[certification]**. What it actually means is that they have practised
> being someone who tries.
>
> **Ask at dinner:** *"What's your first step, and when are you doing it?"*
>
> Then ask them again in two weeks. That single follow-up is the most useful thing any of us can
> do next.
