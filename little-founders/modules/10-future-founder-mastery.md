# Module 10 — Future Founder Mastery

**Icon:** Trophy · **Colour:** Sunshine · **Core badge:** Future Founder · **Sessions:** 4
**Prerequisites:** Module 9 · **Unlocks:** Little Founder (program completion)

> Your story, your evidence, your next step.

## Big idea

The program ends with the learner, not the venture. What did you become good at? What did you
find out about yourself? What are you going to do next — and what is the smallest first step?

**Milestone:** Demo Day closes Session 4.

## Essential questions

- What can I do now that I could not do in Module 1?
- What is my evidence?
- What am I doing next, starting this week?

## Objectives by tier

| | A learner can… |
|---|---|
| **Spark** | Show three things they made, say what they learned, and name one thing they want to try next |
| **Builder** | Assemble a portfolio, present a 3-minute Demo Day talk about their journey, and set a next goal with a first step |
| **Founder** | Run a full retrospective, present a 5-minute Demo Day talk with evidence, make a reasoned continue/pivot/stop decision, and write a 90-day plan |

## Vocabulary

| Term | Plain meaning | Tier |
|---|---|---|
| Learning | Something I can do now that I could not before | Spark |
| Proud | The feeling when you made something hard | Spark |
| Portfolio | The collection that proves what I did | Builder |
| Goal | What I want next, with a date | Builder |
| Retrospective | Looking back to learn on purpose | Founder |
| Pivot | Changing direction, keeping what you learned | Founder |
| 90-day plan | The next three months, in steps | Founder |

## Spark (6–8) — 4 × 45 min

### Session 1 · NOTICE — My best bits

- **Warm-Up (5):** *Remember When.* Guide holds up artifacts from earlier modules; learners call
  out the memory.
- **Mini-Lesson (7):** We are going to show everyone what we did this year.
- **Work (25):** **My Three Things.** Learners choose three pieces from their folder and say why
  each one matters. Helpers scribe the reason onto a label for each.
- **Close (8):** One learner shows their three things to the room.
- **Artifact:** Three chosen pieces with scribed reasons.

### Session 2 · MAKE — My founder book

- **Warm-Up (5):** *Then and Now.* Compare your Module 1 drawing to your Module 9 shop.
- **Mini-Lesson (7):** A book about you: what I made, what I learned, what I want next.
- **Work (25):** Build a four-page founder book — cover with name and drawing, my idea, my best
  bit, next time I will. Brand colours and stickers.
- **Close (8):** Books shown to a partner.
- **Artifact:** Founder book.

### Session 3 · SHOW — Practise the showing

- **Warm-Up (5):** *Big Voice Again.* The Module 5 volume game returns.
- **Mini-Lesson (7):** At Demo Day you show your book and say three sentences: *I made… I learned…
  Next I want to…*
- **Work (25):** Practise in trios with Helpers, three rounds. Set up the display table with
  every learner's work.
- **Close (8):** Invitations home; the room is decorated.
- **Artifact:** Rehearsed three sentences.

### Session 4 · CHANGE + 🏆 DEMO DAY

- **Warm-Up (5):** *Shake It Out.* Nerves game from Module 5.
- **Mini-Lesson (7):** Everyone gets a turn. Everyone claps for everyone.
- **Work (25):** **Demo Day.** Families visit. Each learner shows their book and says their three
  sentences at their own table. Photo booth open with the brand props.
- **Close (8):** Certificates and the **Future Founder** and **Little Founder** badges. Group
  photo under the *Dream · Plan · Build · Grow* signpost.
- **Artifact:** Presented at Demo Day.

## Builder (9–12) — 4 × 60 min

### Session 1 · NOTICE — Retrospective

- **Warm-Up (5):** *Timeline.* Lay out the ten modules; mark the hardest and best moments with
  dots.
- **Mini-Lesson (10):** A retrospective asks three questions: what worked, what did not, what
  would we do differently. About us, not just the product.
- **Work (35):** Team retro on sticky notes across all ten modules, then individually: *three
  things I can do now that I could not in September, with evidence for each.* Evidence must be
  an artifact, not a feeling.
- **Close (10):** Read one "I can now…" statement each.
- **Artifact:** Team retro + three evidenced growth statements.

### Session 2 · MAKE — The portfolio

- **Warm-Up (5):** *Curate.* Given eight pieces, which four tell the best story?
- **Mini-Lesson (10):** A portfolio is chosen, not collected. Six pieces, each with one line:
  what it is, and what it shows about me. Include at least one thing that failed.
- **Work (35):** Assemble the portfolio: problem sheet (M1), plan (M2), cost sheet and price
  defence (M3), poster (M4), pitch recording or script (M5), Startup Report (M9), plus one free
  choice. Write the captions.
- **Close (10):** Swap portfolios; each writes one thing they noticed about the other person's
  growth.
- **Artifact:** Curated portfolio with captions.

### Session 3 · SHOW — Demo Day talk

- **Warm-Up (5):** *Hook Again.* Module 5's ten-second hook, now about you rather than a product.
- **Mini-Lesson (10):** The 3-minute journey talk: where I started, what I built, what went
  wrong, what I learned, what is next. The "what went wrong" section is required, and is usually
  the part the audience remembers.
- **Work (35):** Draft and rehearse in threes with the feedback frame. Two full rounds with a
  timer. Set up the display.
- **Close (10):** One volunteer performs to the whole room.
- **Artifact:** Rehearsed 3-minute talk.

### Session 4 · CHANGE + 🏆 DEMO DAY

- **Warm-Up (5):** *One Deep Breath.*
- **Mini-Lesson (10):** After today, the next goal. Introduce the one-page next-step plan: goal,
  first action, date, who can help.
- **Work (35):** **Demo Day.** Families, staff, and partners attend. Each learner gives their
  3-minute talk; stalls and portfolios on display; photo booth open.
- **Close (10):** Next-step plans written and read to one adult. Certificates, **Future Founder**
  and **Little Founder** badges awarded.
- **Artifact:** Demo Day talk delivered + next-step plan.

## Founder (13–16) — 4 × 90 min

### Session 1 · NOTICE — Full retrospective and self-assessment

- **Warm-Up (10):** *Sealed Envelope.* Reread the goals and predictions you wrote in Modules 1
  and 7.
- **Mini-Lesson (15):** Structured retrospective: keep / drop / try, then a causal pass — for
  each outcome, what decision caused it, and what would you need to have known to decide
  differently?
- **Work (50):** Team retro, then an individual self-assessment against the program's ten
  outcomes with evidence for each. Where evidence is missing, say so rather than claiming it.
  Then a skills inventory: strongest, weakest, most surprising.
- **Close (15):** State your weakest area out loud and what you would do about it.
- **Artifact:** Retro output + evidenced self-assessment.

### Session 2 · MAKE — The decision and the 90-day plan

- **Warm-Up (10):** *Sunk Cost.* Two scenarios where the honest answer is to stop. Argue them.
- **Mini-Lesson (15):** Continue, pivot, or stop — the criteria: is there demand, can the
  economics work, do you want to. Any of the three is a success if reasoned. Then the 90-day
  plan: one goal, three milestones, weekly first action, the resources and people needed, and
  what would make you reconsider.
- **Work (50):** Write the decision with reasoning against evidence from Module 9. Then write the
  90-day plan — for the venture if continuing, for a new direction if pivoting, or for what they
  are taking forward if stopping. Peer challenge in pairs: "what is the weakest assumption in
  this plan?"
- **Close (15):** Read the first action you will take this week. It must be doable within seven
  days.
- **Artifact:** Reasoned decision + 90-day plan.

### Session 3 · SHOW — Demo Day preparation

- **Warm-Up (10):** *Two Versions.* Tell your story in 60 seconds, then in 10.
- **Mini-Lesson (15):** The 5-minute founder talk: the venture in one line, the number that
  matters, the thing that went wrong, what you learned, the decision, the ask. End with a real
  ask of the room — a contact, advice, a customer, a collaborator.
- **Work (50):** Build slides (max 6, brand-consistent), rehearse twice on video, self-assess
  against the Module 5 rubric, and revise. Finalise the portfolio: canvas, unit economics,
  pitch video, Venture Report, community plan, self-assessment, 90-day plan.
- **Close (15):** Peer rehearsal panel; each learner receives one strength and one fix.
- **Artifact:** Final deck + rehearsed talk + complete portfolio.

### Session 4 · CHANGE + 🏆 DEMO DAY

- **Warm-Up (10):** *The Ask.* Rehearse your ask one final time — this is the line people forget.
- **Mini-Lesson (15):** Demo Day conduct: arrive early, know your slot, listen to every other
  talk, follow up with anyone who responds to your ask within 48 hours.
- **Work (50):** **Demo Day.** Public audience — families, mentors, partners, local business
  people. Five-minute talks with Q&A. Portfolios displayed. Photo booth open.
- **Close (15):** Certificates, **Future Founder** and **Little Founder** badges, and the
  alumni invitation — returning as a Mentor or Helper for the next cohort. Follow-up
  commitments written down before anyone leaves.
- **Artifact:** Demo Day talk delivered + follow-up commitments.

## Demo Day — running it

**Format (90–120 minutes)**

| Time | What |
|---|---|
| 0:00 | Doors, photo booth open, portfolios on display |
| 0:15 | Welcome — one Guide, three minutes maximum |
| 0:20 | Talks, in tier order, youngest first |
| 1:10 | Break, browse portfolios, families talk to learners |
| 1:25 | Badge and certificate ceremony |
| 1:45 | Group photo under the signpost; close |

**Rules**

- Every learner presents. Access routes from Module 5 apply in full.
- No ranking, no winner, no prizes for best business. Badges are criterion-based.
- Adults may ask questions; adults may not answer for a learner.
- Photography only against the consent list; a clearly marked no-photo zone is available.
- The Guide's welcome does not explain the program. Let the work do it.

**Invitations:** families, partners from Module 8, mentors, staff, and the next cohort's
learners — the last group matters most for building the program's culture.

## Evidence & badge criteria

| Badge | Criteria | Points |
|---|---|---|
| **Future Founder** (core) | Presented their journey at Demo Day with evidence, and named a next step | 25 |
| **Little Founder** | Completed all ten modules and holds a portfolio | 50 |
| **Show & Tell** | Fifth presentation completed (usually here) | 25 |
| **Keep Building** | Portfolio includes a failure and what was learned from it | 25 |
| **Streak Star** | Attended and contributed across a sustained run (see badge rules) | 25 |

Session completion: 10 Little Points each (40 for the module). Demo Day: 50 points.

**Rubric — mastery:**

| | Emerging | Secure | Strong |
|---|---|---|---|
| Self-knowledge | "It was good" | Names what they learned | Names it, evidences it, and names what they are still bad at |
| Evidence | Assertions | Portfolio supports the claims | Every claim traceable to an artifact, including the failures |
| Communication | Reads a script | Clear, structured, on time | Holds the room, handles questions, makes a real ask |
| Forward step | No plan | A goal | A goal, a first action within seven days, and a named person to help |

## Differentiation & access

- **All Module 5 access routes apply** — pre-recorded, signed, partner-read, one-person
  audience. Every route earns full **Future Founder** credit.
- **Learners who did not complete every module** (joined late, absence): award **Future Founder**
  on the evidence they do hold. **Little Founder** requires all ten modules and may be completed
  in a later cohort — say so warmly, not as a penalty.
- **Portfolios for non-writers:** photographs, recordings, and scribed captions throughout.
- **Families who cannot attend:** record the talk and send it. Arrange a proxy audience member so
  no learner presents to an empty chair.
- **Extension:** Present at a wider event — a governors' meeting, a local business network, the
  next cohort's launch.

## Guide watch-outs

- **Turning Demo Day into a competition.** The moment there is a winner, the quiet learner who
  grew the most disappears. Hold the line on this even when a well-meaning sponsor offers a prize
  — redirect them to fund materials or mentoring instead.
- **Polishing away the failures.** A portfolio with no failures is a weaker portfolio and gets
  marked down against the rubric. Say this in Session 2, before curation.
- **The plan nobody does.** A 90-day plan whose first action cannot start this week is a wish.
  Push until the first action is small and dated.
- **Parents answering questions.** Brief families in the invitation: the young people answer.
- **The flat feeling afterwards.** Common and normal. Plan the alumni route — Helper, Mentor,
  next cohort — before the final session so there is somewhere for the energy to go.
- **Guides who talk too long at the ceremony.** Three minutes. Set a timer and give it to a
  learner.

## Materials

Portfolio folders or boxes · caption labels · display tables and stands · certificates
(name + tier) · badge stickers and pin badges · photo booth props and frame · projector and
laptop (Founder) · timer · camera · alumni invitation slips · next-step plan templates.

## Home extension — 10 minutes

**Spark:** *Show Your Book.* Read your founder book to someone at home. Ask them what their
favourite page is.

**Builder:** *The First Step.* Tell someone at home your next goal and the first action. Ask them
to check in with you in two weeks.

**Founder:** *Send the Follow-Up.* Contact one person who responded to your Demo Day ask, within
48 hours, with a specific request.

## Family note (send after Demo Day)

> Your child has finished Little Founders. Over ten modules they found a problem, built
> something, priced it, marketed it, pitched it, led a team, ran the numbers, worked with their
> community, sold to real customers, and stood up in front of a room to tell the truth about how
> it went.
>
> The badge on their certificate says **Future Founder**. What it actually means is that they
> have practised being someone who tries.
>
> **Ask at dinner:** *"What's your first step, and when are you doing it?"*
>
> Then ask them again in two weeks. That single follow-up is the most useful thing any of us can
> do next.
