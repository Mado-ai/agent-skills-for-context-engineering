# Scope & Sequence

42 sessions per tier. Ten core courses. One cycle — Notice, Make, Show, Change — repeated until
it becomes a habit.

## The 10 × 4 matrix

Read a row to see how one concept spirals across tiers; read a column to see one tier's whole
year.

| Course | 🌱 Little Sprouts (5–7) | 🔨 Junior Builders (8–10) | 🚀 Young Founders (11–13) | 👑 Founder CEOs (14–17) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **1 Idea Starter** | Notice ten annoying things; draw a fix | Problem hunt; pick one with three named sufferers | Interview 8 people; write a validated problem statement | Interview strangers; separate the problem from the favourite solution |
| **2 Business Basics** | Trading game: what do people want? | Gives and gets; what we need to make 20 | One-page plan; competitor scan; test a core claim | Business model canvas; positioning; test the riskiest assumption |
| **3 Money Smarts** | Coins, jars, spend · save · share | Cost of one, set a price, count the takings | Profit, margin, forecast, and a price test | Unit economics, break-even, ledger, cash vs. profit |
| **4 Marketing Magic** | Make a sign someone understands | Know your customer; name, logo, poster | Positioning, two channels, measure the response | Segment insight, A/B test, cost per customer |
| **5 Pitch Like a Pro** | Three sentences to a friendly grown-up | 60 seconds with a prop, to another class | 3-minute pitch with proof, to a visiting panel | 5-minute pitch + Q&A, on video, to outside judges |
| **6 Leadership Lab** | Taking turns, jobs on a team, kind words | Roles, team agreement, running a stand-up | Rotating leadership, conflict, decision-making | Delegation, decision rights, hard conversations |
| **7 Young CEO** | The person who decides and checks | Track three numbers weekly | Goals, dashboard, one decision from data | Metrics that matter, weekly review, add/cut/keep |
| **8 Community Builder** | Who helps our business? Say thank you | Partner with one local person or class | Stakeholders, ethics, a measurable give-back | Externalities, negotiated terms, impact reporting |
| **9 Startup Challenge** | Class shop for one afternoon | Market Day stall, $50 float | Validated venture, $100 float, real customers | Two-week venture, $150 float, full ledger |
| **10 Future Founder Mastery** | Show what I made; what I want next | Portfolio + Demo Day talk; next goal | Retro, portfolio, decision, next-term plan | Retro, continue/pivot/stop, 90-day plan |

## Prerequisites and unlocks

The Learning Map gates courses to keep the sequence honest — Money Smarts before you price
something, Pitch before you sell to strangers.

```
1 Idea Starter ─┐
2 Business Basics ─┤
3 Money Smarts ─┼──▶ 6 Leadership Lab ──▶ 7 Young CEO ──▶ 8 Community Builder
4 Marketing Magic ─┤     (needs 1–5 complete                    │
5 Pitch Like a Pro ─┘     + 250 Little Points)                  ▼
                                              9 Startup Challenge ──▶ 10 Future Founder Mastery
```

| Course | Unlock condition |
|---|---|
| 1–5 | Open from day one. 1 → 2 → 3 is the only hard chain |
| 6 Leadership Lab | Courses 1–5 complete **and** 250 Little Points |
| 7 Young CEO | Course 6 complete |
| 8 Community Builder | Course 7 complete |
| 9 Startup Challenge | Courses 1–8 complete; **Money Smart** and **I Can Pitch!** badges held |
| 10 Future Founder Mastery | Course 9 complete |

The two badge prerequisites on Course 9 are non-negotiable from Junior Builders upward: no
learner handles money or sells to the public without having demonstrated pricing and pitching.
If a learner is short, run the Course 3 and Course 5 catch-up activities rather than holding the
whole cohort.

## Session shape

Every session, every tier, runs the same four blocks. Times: Sprouts / Builders / Founders / CEOs.

| Block | Time | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| **Warm-Up** | 5 / 5 / 8 / 10 | A game or question that plants today's idea before it is named |
| **Mini-Lesson** | 7 / 10 / 12 / 15 | Guide talks. Hard cap — if it runs long, cut it, not the Work |
| **Work** | 25 / 35 / 42 / 50 | Learners make, test, calculate, or interview. The centre of the session |
| **Close** | 8 / 10 / 13 / 15 | Share one thing, log it, award points, name what comes next |

The Mini-Lesson cap matters. The most common failure mode in this program is a Guide explaining
entrepreneurship for 25 minutes to children who have not yet made anything.

## Assessment rhythm

| When | What |
|---|---|
| Every session | Close-out log entry (one line) + Little Points |
| Every course, Session 3 | Peer feedback using the three-part frame |
| Every course, Session 4 | Badge check against published criteria; portfolio piece filed |
| After Courses 3, 6, 9 | Guide's short written note to family — progress, not grades |
| Course 10 | Portfolio review + Demo Day + self-assessment → **tier certification** |

There are no tests. Evidence is what the learner made and what they can say about it. Rubrics in
[04-assessment-and-badges.md](04-assessment-and-badges.md).
