# Scope & Sequence

42 sessions per tier. Ten modules. One cycle — Notice, Make, Show, Change — repeated until it
becomes a habit.

## The 10 × 3 matrix

What each module asks of each tier. Read a row to see how one concept spirals; read a column
to see a tier's whole year.

| Module | Spark (6–8) | Builder (9–12) | Founder (13–16) |
|---|---|---|---|
| **1 Idea Starter** | Notice 10 annoying things at home; draw a fix | Problem hunt in school; pick one with 3 named sufferers | Problem interviews with 5 strangers; write a problem statement |
| **2 Business Basics** | Trading game: what do people want, what will they give? | Value exchange + one-page plan; product vs. business | Business model canvas; competitor scan; test a core assumption |
| **3 Money Smarts** | Coins, saving, "money in / money out" jars | Costs, price, profit; set a price and defend it | Unit economics, break-even, a real ledger, cash vs. profit |
| **4 Marketing Magic** | Make a sign someone will notice | Know your customer; message, poster, name, logo | Positioning, channel test, measure response rate |
| **5 Pitch Like a Pro** | 3 sentences to a friendly grown-up | 90-second pitch with a prop, to another class | 5-minute pitch + Q&A, on video, to outside judges |
| **6 Leadership Lab** | Taking turns, jobs on a team, kind words | Roles, a team agreement, running a stand-up | Delegation, conflict, decision-making under disagreement |
| **7 Young CEO** | The person who decides and checks | Track 3 numbers weekly; make one decision from data | Dashboard, weekly review, hire/fire a feature, own the plan |
| **8 Community Builder** | Who helps our business? Say thank you | Partner with one local person or class | Stakeholders, ethics, give-back plan, a real partnership |
| **9 Startup Challenge** | Class shop for one afternoon | Market Day stall with a $50 float | Two-week venture with real customers and a real ledger |
| **10 Future Founder Mastery** | Show what I made; what I want to try next | Portfolio + Demo Day talk; set a next goal | Retrospective, decision to continue/pivot/stop, 90-day plan |

## Prerequisites and unlocks

The Learning Map gates modules to keep the sequence honest — Money Smarts before you price
something, Pitch before you sell to strangers.

```
1 Idea Starter ─┐
2 Business Basics ─┤
3 Money Smarts ─┼──▶ 6 Leadership Lab ──▶ 7 Young CEO ──▶ 8 Community Builder
4 Marketing Magic ─┤     (needs 1–5 complete                    │
5 Pitch Like a Pro ─┘     + 250 Little Points)                  ▼
                                              9 Startup Challenge ──▶ 10 Future Founder Mastery
```

| Module | Unlock condition |
|---|---|
| 1–5 | Open from day one. Recommended in order, but 1 → 2 → 3 is the only hard chain. |
| 6 Leadership Lab | Modules 1–5 complete **and** 250 Little Points |
| 7 Young CEO | Module 6 complete |
| 8 Community Builder | Module 7 complete |
| 9 Startup Challenge | Modules 1–8 complete; **Money Smart** and **I Can Pitch!** badges held |
| 10 Future Founder Mastery | Module 9 complete |

The two badge prerequisites on Module 9 are non-negotiable at Builder and Founder tiers: a
learner should not handle money or sell to the public without having demonstrated pricing and
pitching. If a learner is short, run the Module 3 and Module 5 catch-up activities rather than
holding the whole cohort.

## Session shape

Every session, every tier, runs the same four blocks. Times shown Spark / Builder / Founder.

| Block | Time | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| **Warm-Up** | 5 / 5 / 10 min | A game or question that plants today's idea before it is named |
| **Mini-Lesson** | 7 / 10 / 15 min | Guide talks. Hard cap — if it runs long, cut it, not the Work |
| **Work** | 25 / 35 / 50 min | Learners make, test, calculate, or interview. The centre of the session |
| **Close** | 8 / 10 / 15 min | Share one thing, log it, award points, name what comes next |

The Mini-Lesson cap matters. The most common failure mode in this program is a Guide who
explains entrepreneurship for 25 minutes to children who have not yet made anything.

## Assessment rhythm

| When | What |
|---|---|
| Every session | Close-out log entry (one line) + Little Points |
| Every module, Session 3 | Peer feedback using the three-part frame |
| Every module, Session 4 | Badge check against published criteria; portfolio piece filed |
| After Modules 3, 6, 9 | Guide's short written note to family — progress, not grades |
| Module 10 | Portfolio review + Demo Day performance + self-assessment |

There are no tests. Evidence is what the learner made and what they can say about it. Full
rubrics in [04-assessment-and-badges.md](04-assessment-and-badges.md).
