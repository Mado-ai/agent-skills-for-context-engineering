# Standards & Safeguarding

This program puts children in front of real customers and, at two tiers, real money. That is
what makes it work, and it is also what makes this document non-optional reading before
Module 3.

Nothing here overrides your setting's own policies. Where this document and your safeguarding,
health and safety, or data protection policy differ, **your policy wins** — and if your policy
is silent, treat this as the minimum.

## Safeguarding

### Adult supervision

| Tier | Ratio | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Spark | 1 adult : 6 learners | Plus one adult on money at any trading event |
| Builder | 1 : 8 | Plus one adult on money |
| Founder | 1 : 12 | Plus one adult on money |

All adults — including parent volunteers, mentors, and alumni Helpers — must have the
background checks your setting requires **before** any contact with learners. An unchecked
mentor may attend only as a member of a public audience.

### Contact with people outside the setting

Modules 8, 9, and 10 involve real external contact. Rules:

- No learner is ever alone with an adult who is not part of the setting's checked staff.
- All partnership approaches, visits, and calls happen with a checked adult present.
- No exchange of personal contact details between a learner and an external adult. All
  follow-up runs through the setting's address.
- Visits off-site follow your setting's trip procedure, including consent and risk assessment.
- Mentors are briefed in writing: their role is questions and encouragement, they do not take
  learners' contact details, and they do not offer employment, investment, or commercial
  arrangements.

### Photography, video, and publishing

- Maintain a **consent list** for the cohort — photo, video, internal use, external/social use,
  each consented separately. Check it before every recording, not at the point of publishing.
- Module 5 Founder tier requires video for self-assessment. A learner without consent
  self-assesses from an audio recording or from peer notes. No exceptions, no pressure.
- Demo Day has a clearly marked no-photography zone.
- Never publish a learner's surname, school, class, or location alongside their image.

### Online activity

Any online channel used in Module 4 or Module 9 requires a signed **online conduct agreement**
covering:

- The account is created, owned, and monitored by a staff member. Learners never hold the
  credentials.
- No surnames, no school name, no uniform, no location, no faces without consent.
- No direct messaging with the public. Comments moderated or turned off.
- Any contact from a stranger is reported to the Guide immediately and not answered.
- The account is closed or archived at the end of the program.

The **Digital Safe** badge is awarded for meeting all of this — it is a real standard, not a
participation sticker.

### Money-related safeguarding

Talking about money is not neutral for every family.

- **Never use a learner's family finances as an example.** All scenarios are business
  scenarios.
- Watch for disclosures. A child who says "we can't afford that" in a pricing discussion may be
  telling you something that needs following up through your safeguarding route.
- No learner contributes their own or their family's money to a venture. Floats come from the
  program.
- Anything a learner keeps from proceeds follows the group's split rule, agreed in advance in
  Module 3, and is the same for everyone regardless of individual sales.
- If a learner is anxious about spending or losing money, they may take a non-money role
  without explanation to the group.

## Handling real money

Applies from Module 3 (Builder and Founder) and throughout Module 9.

**The four rules**

1. **Two learners count, one adult witnesses.** All three initial the sheet. Every count, every
   time.
2. **Count at three fixed points** — open, midpoint, close. Never reconstruct a count later.
3. **The tin is never unattended and never held by one person alone.** It lives in a named,
   locked place between sessions.
4. **A discrepancy is investigated, never adjusted.** Write down the actual figure and the gap,
   then look at the process.

**When money goes missing.** It is almost always a process failure — miscounted float, unlogged
purchase, change given wrong. Investigate the process out loud, in front of everyone, without
naming anyone. Never question a child about theft; that is a matter for your setting's
procedures and its designated safeguarding lead, not for a business lesson.

**Float limits.** Builder $50 per team. Founder set by the setting; recommend $150 per team
maximum. The float is signed out and signed back.

**Proceeds.** Governed by the group's split rule from Module 3 — typically reinvest / share /
give back. Decided by the group before Market Day, recorded, and honoured even when the result
disappoints someone. Where the setting requires proceeds to go to a charity or fund, say so at
the start of Module 3, not after learners have imagined otherwise.

## Trading, legal, and practical checks

Run these in **Module 9, Session 1**. Discovering a blocker the week of Market Day is a
preventable failure.

| Check | Who confirms | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Permission to trade on the premises | Setting leadership | Some sites need written approval |
| Local rules on trading by minors | Guide | Varies widely; check before promising a public sale |
| Food handling and allergen rules | Guide + setting policy | Home-baked goods are prohibited in many settings. Check in S1 — this can end a venture concept |
| Allergen labelling | Team | Required for any food, even where home baking is allowed |
| Product safety for anything worn, small, or for young children | Guide | No small parts for under-3 audiences; no electricals |
| Insurance / liability cover for a public event | Setting | Usually covered by existing policy; confirm |
| Data — any customer names, emails, or sign-ups | Guide | Minimise collection, store on the setting's systems, delete after the program. Never on a learner's device |
| Risk assessment for Market Day and Demo Day | Guide | Per setting template; includes crowd, stalls, power, weather |
| Cash security | Guide | Route and storage agreed in advance |

**Things this program does not do:** register companies, open bank accounts in a learner's
name, take deposits for goods that do not yet exist beyond a supervised pre-order, sell online
to the public without staff-owned accounts, or make claims about earnings to families.

## Inclusion

- **Cost never gates participation.** No learner supplies their own materials. If a home
  extension needs anything beyond a pencil, it has been written wrongly.
- **Every assessment route has a non-writing, non-public-speaking equivalent** carrying identical
  credit — see [04-assessment-and-badges.md](04-assessment-and-badges.md).
- **Teams are assigned, not chosen**, at Builder and Founder tiers. Self-selection reliably
  leaves the same children last.
- **Roles rotate.** Nobody is permanently the money person, and nobody is permanently the one
  who tidies.
- **Public presentation is never compulsory in a single format.** Options are offered to
  everyone, so no learner is visibly opted out.
- **Language.** In Module 8, avoid framing give-back as charity from a position above; some
  learners' families are on the receiving end of the goodwill being discussed.

## Emotional safety

Module 9 is genuinely stressful and Module 10 is emotionally exposing. Expect and plan for it.

- Name the feelings before the numbers in Module 9, Session 5. Some cohorts cry; this is
  normal and not a sign the module went wrong.
- Never compare teams publicly on takings. Badges are criterion-based precisely so that the
  team that lost money can still hold every badge.
- A learner who wants to withdraw from a public-facing role on the day may do so, immediately,
  without justifying it. Have a behind-the-scenes role ready.
- Plan the after: the alumni route (Helper, Mentor, next cohort) should be announced at Demo
  Day so the energy has somewhere to go.

## Pre-program checklist

Complete before Session 1 of Module 1:

- [ ] Adult ratios confirmed for every planned session and event
- [ ] All adults background-checked per policy
- [ ] Consent list created — photo, video, internal, external
- [ ] Online conduct agreement drafted, ready for Module 4
- [ ] Money handling procedure agreed with setting leadership, ready for Module 3
- [ ] Float source and limits confirmed
- [ ] Split rule constraints known (does the setting require proceeds to go anywhere?)
- [ ] Market Day date, venue, and permissions provisionally booked
- [ ] Food, trading, and insurance questions raised with leadership
- [ ] Risk assessment templates located
- [ ] Safeguarding lead briefed that the program includes money conversations and external
      contact
- [ ] Family letter sent explaining what the program involves, including trading and photography
