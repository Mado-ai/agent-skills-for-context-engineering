## Photo booth props

Used at Idea Fair, Market Day, and Demo Day. Make once from foam board; they last for years.

Speech bubbles on sticks: *CEO of my future* · *Problem Solver* · *Ideas Change the World* ·
*Future Leader* · *I have a BIG IDEA!*
Plus: giant frame with *Building tomorrow, today* · star glasses · branded cap · a rocket or
lightbulb cut-out backdrop.

Photography only against the consent list.

## Reuse and sustainability

The **Green Founder** badge is undermined by a program that generates waste, so:

- Prototypes use recycled cardboard and packaging. Ask families to donate at the start of M1.
- Stall signage is reused across cohorts where it is not team-specific.
- Leftover Market Day stock is logged, then sold at a later event, donated, or used by the next
  cohort — never quietly binned.
- Portfolios are paper folders, not plastic wallets.
- Print double-sided; the templates are designed for it.

## Substitutions

| If you do not have | Use instead |
|---|---|
| Spreadsheet access | Paper trackers and a calculator — fully valid at every tier |
| A printer | Hand-drawn templates on flip chart, copied once and photographed |
| A public venue for Market Day | An internal event for other classes and staff |
| Permission to handle real money | An internal token economy; substitute token reconciliation for money reconciliation |
| Local businesses to partner with | The school office, another year group, a library, a sports club, a supervised online community |
| Video equipment | Audio recording, or peer observation notes |
