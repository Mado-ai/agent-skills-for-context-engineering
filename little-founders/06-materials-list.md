## Templates to prepare

One page each unless noted. Reproduce in brand type and colour. Little Sprouts versions are
picture-first with eight words or fewer. Bilingual cohorts need both languages at Little Sprouts
and Junior Builders; from Young Founders up, learners may choose one.

**Course 1** — Bug Hunt sheet (Sprouts) · Problem Hunt sheet (Builders) · interview script
template (Founders, CEOs) · *My Big Idea* page · feedback face cards · validation brief · idea
brief

**Course 2** — Two-arrow give/get page (Sprouts) · three-test sheet · input list with bottleneck
(Builders) · one-page plan (Founders) · business model canvas at A3 (CEOs) · competitor scan

**Course 3** — Paper jars and three envelopes (Sprouts) · cost sheet · price-test recording sheet ·
forecast table · takings sheet · ledger template · six-week cash plan (CEOs)

**Course 4** — Customer sketch · brand sheet · three-second test tally · channel results sheet ·
A/B test recording sheet (CEOs)

**Course 5** — Three-sentence card (Sprouts) · four-box pitch planner (Builders) · five-box planner
(Founders, CEOs) · judge rubric sheet · self-assessment sheet

**Course 6** — Job cards (Sprouts: Maker, Teller, Timekeeper, Tidier) · role cards (Builders: Lead,
Maker, Money, Voice) · team agreement · scenario cards · stand-up format card · rotation schedule ·
decision map · premortem sheet · operating agreement

**Course 7** — Picture tally chart (Sprouts) · tracker (paper and spreadsheet) · review question
card · decision card · decision record · dashboard template

**Course 8** — Helper web (Sprouts) · thank-you card blanks · three-ring stakeholder map ·
partnership ask · one-page partnership agreement · power/interest grid · decision record form ·
community plan

**Course 9** — Market Day plan · production tracker · takings sheet · float sign-out sheet · kit
checklist · 20-minute observation log · Startup Report (Builders) · Venture Report (Founders, CEOs)

**Course 10** — Founder book, 4 pages (Sprouts) · portfolio caption labels · retro sheet ·
next-step plan (Builders) · next-term plan (Founders) · 90-day plan (CEOs) · certificates in four
designs

**Program-wide** — session log page · Guide's assessment record · family note template · consent
list · online conduct agreement

## Photo booth props

Used at Idea Fair, Market Day, and Demo Day. Make once from foam board; they last for years.

Speech bubbles on sticks: *CEO of my future* · *Problem Solver* · *Ideas Change the World* ·
*Future Leader* · *I have a BIG IDEA!*
Plus: a giant frame reading *Building tomorrow, today* · star glasses · branded cap · a rocket or
lightbulb cut-out backdrop.

Photography only against the consent list.

## Reuse and sustainability

The **Green Founder** badge is undermined by a program that generates waste, so:

- Prototypes use recycled cardboard and packaging. Ask families to donate at the start of Course 1.
- Stall signage is reused across cohorts where it is not team-specific.
- Leftover Market Day stock is logged, then sold at a later event, donated, or used by the next
  cohort — never quietly binned.
- Portfolios are paper folders, not plastic wallets.
- Print double-sided; the templates are designed for it.

## Substitutions

| If you do not have | Use instead |
|---|---|
| Spreadsheet access | Paper trackers and a calculator — fully valid at every tier |
| A printer | Hand-drawn templates on flip chart, copied once and photographed |
| A public venue for Market Day | An internal event for other classes and staff |
| Permission to handle real money | An internal token economy; substitute token reconciliation for money reconciliation |
| Local businesses to partner with | The school office, another division, a library, a rink, a sports club, a supervised online community |
| Video equipment | Audio recording, or peer observation notes |
| A francophone Guide for a bilingual cohort | A bilingual Helper or an older bilingual learner as language partner; do not run a bilingual cohort with no French-speaking adult present |
