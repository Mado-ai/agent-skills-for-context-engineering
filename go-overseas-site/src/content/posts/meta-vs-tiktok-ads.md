If you're spending on paid social, the question comes up fast: **Meta ads or TikTok ads?**
Both can drive real results — but they reward very different approaches. Here's how to choose
in 2026.

## How they're different

**Meta (Facebook & Instagram)** is the workhorse of performance marketing: mature targeting,
deep audiences across every age group, and ad formats built for direct response and
retargeting. **TikTok** is built for discovery — native, entertaining video that can reach
huge new audiences fast, especially under 35.

| Factor | Meta | TikTok |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Audience | Broad, all ages | Younger-skewing, growing fast |
| Strength | Retargeting & conversions | Awareness & discovery |
| Creative | Image, carousel, video | Native short-form video |
| Buying intent | Higher down-funnel | Higher top-funnel |

## When to lean on Meta

Choose Meta first if you need **measurable conversions now**, have an existing audience to
retarget, or sell to an older or B2B-leaning customer. Its optimization and attribution are
still the most reliable for bottom-of-funnel results.

## When to lean on TikTok

Choose TikTok if you have a **visual, demonstrable product**, want to reach new audiences, and
can produce native video that doesn't feel like an ad. The platform rewards authenticity over
polish — a phone-shot demo often beats a glossy studio spot.

## The honest answer: most brands run both

In practice, the platforms work best together. TikTok creates demand and feeds the top of the
funnel; Meta captures and converts it with retargeting. The key is **creative built for each
platform** — recycling the same asset everywhere is the fastest way to waste budget.

## Frequently asked questions

**Which is cheaper?** TikTok often has lower CPMs, but Meta typically converts better
down-funnel. Compare on cost *per result*, not cost per impression.

**How much should I budget to test?** Enough to exit the learning phase — generally a few
weeks of consistent spend per platform before judging results.
