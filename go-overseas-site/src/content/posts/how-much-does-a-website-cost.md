"How much does a website cost?" is the first question almost every business asks — and the
honest answer is *it depends*. But "it depends" isn't useful when you're trying to budget. So
here are real 2026 price ranges, what drives them, and how to spend smart.

## What you're actually paying for

A website price is rarely just design. It bundles strategy, copywriting, design,
development, integrations, testing, and launch — plus ongoing hosting and maintenance. The
biggest cost driver isn't the number of pages; it's **complexity**: custom design, custom
functionality, and integrations with tools like a CRM, booking system, or payment provider.

## Website cost ranges in 2026

| Type | Typical range (CAD) | Best for |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Template landing page | $1,000–$3,000 | Validating an offer, a single campaign |
| Custom marketing site (5–10 pages) | $5,000–$15,000 | Most small & mid-sized businesses |
| E-commerce store | $8,000–$30,000+ | Selling products online |
| Web app / custom platform | $25,000–$100,000+ | Software, portals, booking systems |

These are ballparks. A brochure site for a local service business sits at the lower end; a
booking platform with user accounts and payments sits much higher.

## One-time vs. ongoing costs

Don't budget only for the build. Plan for:

- **Hosting & domain** — often $0–$50/month for a marketing site.
- **Maintenance & updates** — security, plugins, content changes.
- **Growth** — SEO, new landing pages, A/B tests, and CRO over time.

A cheap site that's never updated quietly loses you traffic and leads. The site is an asset,
not a one-off purchase.

## How to get the most for your budget

1. **Lead with goals, not pages.** "Book more consultations" produces a very different (and
   cheaper) site than "look impressive."
2. **Invest in the homepage and top 3 pages.** That's where most visitors decide.
3. **Build for SEO from day one** — fast load times, clean structure, and real content beat a
   redesign later.
4. **Pick a partner who owns the outcome**, not just the pixels.

## Frequently asked questions

**Is a template or custom site better?** Templates are fast and affordable for simple needs.
Custom wins when design, performance, and conversion directly affect revenue.

**Why do quotes vary so much?** Scope, custom functionality, content, and who's doing the work.
Always compare *what's included*, not just the bottom-line number.
