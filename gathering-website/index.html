The seat count is the product

A buffet gets worse the moment it gets crowded.

So we cap it. Between 32 and 48 people per service, ticketed, never overbooked. That single decision changes everything downstream: the food is replaced on a rhythm instead of a rush, there is no queue at the carving board, and you can hear the person across from you.
