How the seat hold works

Each service reserves a share of the room for members — 35% at breakfast, 50% at dinner — and holds it until 24 hours before the doors open. Guests see the rest. When the hold releases, whatever is left goes public. That is why a service can read “member seats only” to one person and “12 seats open” to another.
