Three businesses, one room, one idea

Gathering started as a catering kitchen that kept getting the same request: can we just eat here? So we gave the room a timetable. Five self-serve buffets across a day, each one ticketed and capped, so a buffet could finally be a calm thing instead of a queue. The coffee house stayed walk-in. The baskets kept going out the back door.

What we cap and why

Scarcity that serves the guest

A room that seats 44 for dinner could seat 80. We know, because we tried it. At 80 the brisket goes out in trays instead of being carved, the salad bowls sit too long, and the last table of the night eats the leftovers of the first. Capping the room is the only way the fifth service is as good as the first.
