⚽ PlayMetrics
How it works

Turn one wide camera into a broadcast — and a data room.

Play Metrics auto-produces your match (virtual cameraman, live graphics), then tracks every player and the ball to deliver possession, heatmaps, physical metrics and automatic highlights — no operator required.

🎥 Auto-production 📊 Possession & heatmaps 🏃 Distance & speed ✂️ Auto highlights 📐 Pitch homography 🧠 Color / YOLO detection
🎥

1 · Capture & auto-produce

Upload a panoramic clip. The virtual camera pans and zooms to follow play and burns in a live scoreboard + possession bar.

📊

2 · Analyze

Computer-vision tracking yields possession %, top-down heatmaps and per-player distance & top speed, calibrated to a real 105×68 m pitch.

✂️

3 · Highlight & review

Exciting moments are detected and cut into clips automatically. Everything lands in your private dashboard.
